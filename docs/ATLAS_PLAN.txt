Atlas representation and delivery plan — 4 November 2026
Status: proposed contract grounded in the source review below; NOT a deployed
schema, a newly extracted atlas, or a completed scientific validation.
This is the current reading entry point for the representation decision.

DECISION

Build a catalogue of measurement methods with source-qualified workflow graphs.
Use Illumina's existing level of detail. Add paper-supported historical change
claims and narrowly scoped refinements. Do not build a universal executable
protocol language or infer ancestry from workflow similarity.

The main defect is semantic organization, not missing prose. Existing records
already have State, Operation, Flow and evidence fields, but their contents are
not consistently classified or named. Adding another miscellaneous description
field would not resolve this. The draft representation.py adds useful evidence
facets but does not yet provide the controlled descriptions specified here.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY EXAMINED

- 50 archived method depictions in 10 structural groups, with an individual
  engineering finding for each in experiments/representation_review_20261104/.
  This is NOT a new full-paper scientific review of all 50 methods.
- The RNA collection (122 PDF pages), DNA collection (146), and the downloaded
  Methods Guide (148; 2020 version 6): contents, relevant explanatory sections,
  method comparisons and selected page images. The reading ledger records pages
  and scope; it does not claim every page or reference was read in full.
- The requested sequencing and kit pages, plus their library-preparation and
  NGS-workflow pages. These describe different levels, not one common ontology.
- Reactome's data model and three official Java model classes; LabOP's paper
  and typed Behavior parameters in official code; EXACT2 and OBI assay-pattern
  publications. These supply representation precedents, not an evaluated atlas
  extractor. No external code was copied or installed.

SOURCE LESSONS THAT CHANGE THE DESIGN

1. The collections' introduction says diagrams omit details, group some methods,
   and are arranged by similarity. References emphasize recent literature. Thus
   source order, adjacency and first-listed references cannot define ancestry.
2. The Methods Guide separates application, library preparation, instrument and
   informatics. Its 2020 products/specifications are historical, not a current
   purchasing list. It also includes arrays: not everything in it is a sequencing
   method. Product, instrument, assay and operation must not be interchangeable.
3. In the RNA collection, G&T-seq (p118) and scM&T-seq (p119) share separation and
   RNA processing, while the DNA branch changes. These are useful explicit change
   leads before any graph-matching algorithm. They still need primary-source
   verification for historical publication.
4. The ChIP-family page (DNA p104) groups names and depicts exonuclease digestion.
   Do not assign every depicted operation and performance claim to every member.
5. Drop-seq's endpoint caption (RNA p110) names the biological target where our
   graph needs the actual readout. Literal captions must remain recoverable but
   cannot be the sole scientific label authority.
6. cP-RNA-seq (RNA p14) depicts several end-chemistry classes coexisting in a
   mixture; they are not automatically isolated fractions. Molecular properties
   and physical routing must be represented separately.
7. Smart-seq/Smart-seq2 (RNA p97) describes changes in reagents and conditions
   despite a largely shared schematic. A condition belongs in the atlas when it
   defines the comparison or interface; there is no blanket exclusion of buffers.
8. A catalogue's associated kits are leads, not proof of the exact kit used in
   every implementation or of compatibility with every listed assay variant.

ONE VOCABULARY FOR THE OBJECTS

Method: a named assay approach, with aliases and multiple classifications.
Workflow: one explicitly scoped implementation/variant of that Method. It states
  the source and version being described and contains Operations, States, Flows.
Operation: a source-supported action or explicitly grouped procedure acting on
  declared inputs. An enzyme, molecule, purpose or instrument is not an Operation.
State: the material or data at a particular workflow boundary. It describes what
  is there, in what consequential form and organization. It is not an action.
Flow: an evidenced directed connection within that Workflow.
Resource: a reagent, kit, device or software used by an Operation. A reagent's
  molecular content may also be a material input; its role is local to the action.
Module: an optional named view over a connected group of Operations. It is not
  a compulsory level, universal stage or automatic reusable scientific component.
Change claim: a separately reviewed relationship between implementations, with
  exact affected operation groups and evidence. It is not an internal Flow.

Different depictions of one method remain separate evidence records. Different
variants remain separate Workflows. Similar Operations remain local occurrences
even when linked to a shared operation concept. IDs identify records; they do not
prove scientific identity. A hash detects changed bytes, not semantic sameness.

METHOD STRATIFICATION — MULTIPLE AXES, NOT ONE EXCLUSIVE TREE

Keep the original Illumina paths verbatim, including multiple paths. Add these
fixed fields to a method profile; do not replace the source categories:

illumina_categories: original category paths and their source locations.
measurements: list of {target_entities, property_or_relationship, biological_unit,
  physical_reporter, observed_signal, inference, evidence}. These distinguish what
  is being studied from what the instrument observes. Each measurement has an ID.
specimen: native specimen and the actual input boundary shown by the Workflow.
joint_measurements: measurement IDs linked at a stated unit, with the mechanism
  or experimental association and evidence. Same cell, same fraction, same
  molecule, same tissue region and separate matched samples are different claims.
workflow_ids: the method's scoped implementations, not source-file duplicates.
resource_requirements: only evidenced requirements, with implementation scope.

Examples of scientific classification to verify against the cited implementation:
- ChIP-seq: protein-associated genomic regions; selected DNA is sequenced. This
  does not mean protein sequence or protein abundance is directly measured.
- BisChIP-seq family: methylation in DNA selected by a chromatin feature; do not
  call it a general paired proteome-and-methylome assay (DNA collection p101).
- G&T-seq: genome and transcriptome associated with the same cell (RNA p118).
- scM&T-seq: methylome and transcriptome associated with the same cell (RNA p119).
- TRIBE: RNA-binding association inferred through RNA editing (RNA p65).

These profiles allow a method to appear in several catalogue views without
duplicating it. A query can retrieve RNA-protein assays by biological relation,
DNA reporters by carrier, or methylation-plus-RNA assays by linked measurements.
Category membership creates retrieval candidates, not ancestry or compatibility.

The property_or_relationship field must reference a definition, not an unchecked
new string. Initial measurement concepts to test include sequence/variation,
abundance, chemical modification, accessibility, physical association, molecular
structure, synthesis during an interval, decay/turnover inference and replication.
They are not interchangeable: RNA abundance is not RNA synthesis, and methylation
is a property of DNA rather than a second molecule called 'methylation'. The
biological unit and inference fields prevent same-cell and same-molecule claims
from collapsing. New concepts use the same reviewed proposal path as Operations.

STATE CONTRACT — FIXED SLOTS, OPEN SCIENTIFIC VALUES

Keep kind=material|data, but stop placing every property in features:[prose].
Use a tagged payload: material fields cannot be used as data fields or vice versa.
Every assertion retains evidence. The display label is rendered from these facts
and checked against them; source_label is preserved separately when available.

Material payload:
  components: scientifically relevant constituents/classes. Each has a local ID,
    entity_type, name and optional verified external identifier. Starting entity
    types: DNA, RNA, protein, small_molecule, cell, nucleus, tissue,
    biological_mixture, material_support, other. 'Other' requires a term proposal.
    Do not enumerate every molecular constituent of a cell or a buffer.
  molecular_features: component ID, property, value and evidence. Initial
    properties: strandedness, topology, terminal_group, modification,
    sequence_segment. Chemical site/end is explicit when relevant. cDNA is DNA
    with a provenance/identity description, not a new fundamental substance type.
  associations: relations among named constituents/supports/compartments, using
    explicit relations such as hybridized_to, covalently_linked_to, bound_to,
    contained_in or co_present_with. Co-presence does not assert binding.
  partition: identifies an independent fraction, intact compartment collection,
    or pooled material when supported. Multiple molecular classes alone do not
    establish separate fractions. Do not infer literal well/cell counts from icons.
  attribution: what is associated with what (molecule, cell, sample, location,
    time window, contact), and the physical or experimental carrier of the link.

Data payload:
  data_type: sequence_reads, alignment, instrument_signal, measurement_table,
    derived_result or other (with a definition).
  content: recorded observations/identifiers and the entities they represent.
  attribution: associations actually recoverable in that dataset and their basis.

Field completeness is assessed explicitly as recorded, not_reported,
not_applicable (with a reason), or unresolved. 'Not reported' is not evidence that
a property is absent. False/absent and unchanged are scientific claims requiring
support. A missing material description must not become an invented DNA library.

A State can contain an RNA-DNA complex or a mixed population. It can also be the
prepared input to a procedure whose earlier preparation is outside scope. It
cannot replace an action that is necessary to explain an in-scope transition.

Example, abbreviated design specification rather than a new extraction:
  kind: material
  components: [{id: c1, entity_type: DNA, name: first-strand cDNA}]
  molecular_features:
    - {component: c1, property: strandedness, value: single_stranded}
    - {component: c1, property: terminal_group, site: 3_prime, value: azide}
  source: RNA collection p12; primary verification pending for atlas admission
This permits a query for 3-prime azide-ended cDNA without guessing from its name.

OPERATION CONTRACT — TYPE PLUS ARGUMENTS, NOT ENDLESS METHOD-SPECIFIC NAMES

Required fields:
  action: a registered operation concept or a pending definition proposal.
  inputs: State IDs with local roles, distinguishing processed sample/template,
    reagent, catalyst, capture support, data and reference data when supported.
  outputs: State IDs, with independent outputs distinguished from classes in one
    mixture. A common caption does not join independently processed branches.
  effect: the source-supported change, selection or observation, anchored to
    the affected input/output components and relevant property names.
  mechanism: source-supported mechanism and participating resource references.
  purpose: what this action contributes to this assay, when supported.
  resolution: source_block, coupled_action, composite or unresolved_transition.
    A composite is legitimate; it does not require invented internal steps.
  evidence and unresolved questions.

Operation concepts have a preferred verb phrase, definition, aliases, argument
roles, discriminating properties and explicit near-matches/exclusions. Seed from
observed source terminology; map to existing ontology terms only when meanings
agree. Examples: reverse transcription, template switching, restriction digestion,
affinity capture, bisulfite conversion, tagmentation, PCR amplification, pooling,
sequencing and alignment. This is an initial vocabulary, not a claim of completeness.

Do not create 'DNA ligation on beads after ChIP' as a separate fundamental action
type. Represent a join action with DNA substrate, ligation mechanism, end features,
attachment and local workflow role. Conversely, an azide-alkyne click coupling is
not equivalent to an enzyme-mediated ligation merely because both join an adaptor.
An entity such as T4-beta-glucosyltransferase belongs in resources; its supported
action is named separately. Name normalization cannot invent that action.

Registry policy: workers use a versioned vocabulary snapshot, retrieve definitions
and propose additions when needed. They do not directly create accepted global
concepts. Batch admission compares definitions in context, retains unresolved
proposals and commits accepted terms with version checks. Exact duplicate keys can
be locked/uniqued mechanically; semantic duplicates require review. No global lock
around every extraction and no automatic union of similarity-linked definitions.

Conditional requirements make the vocabulary operational:
- Reverse transcription: identify the RNA template and DNA product; record a
  priming/handle detail when supported and relevant to attribution or comparison.
- Ligation/coupling: identify the joined partners, linkage mechanism and relevant
  end groups. An enzyme name alone does not establish the compatible substrate.
- Capture: identify the selected material, recognition basis, support when used,
  and retained output. Do not invent what happened to an unshown residual fraction.
- Chemical conversion: identify which substrate property changes and the readout
  consequence, including controls when part of the source's discrimination logic.
- Pooling: identify what units are combined and whether their contents remain
  compartmentalized. A pooling label cannot resolve this question by itself.
- Sequencing/readout: distinguish the assayed molecule from the emitted data;
  specify the observable used for the biological inference.
These are interpretation obligations. An unreported value becomes an addressed
unknown, not a fabricated required value. Source-limited composite blocks may
defer internal requirements until the specific comparison needs refinement.

GRANULARITY RULE AND THE ROLE OF MODULES

Start with source-supported transformation blocks, not one node per caption/icon.
Keep a source block intact unless it hides independent routing, a consequential
interface, attribution, or the particular difference under investigation.
Use the collection prose alongside the image before requesting a full paper.
When a paper resolves a hidden distinction, add a scoped refinement linked to the
original block and reconcile its boundary inputs/outputs. Do not silently rewrite
all historical versions. A refined child subgraph must preserve the parent's
declared scope and explain any boundary correction.

Retain coupled chemistry as one Operation when that is what the source supports;
record multiple effects. A module name or number of Operations is never a unit of
scientific equivalence. Compare connected operation groups with boundary states.
Do not require every assay to pass through a universal sample-prep/chemistry chain.
Optional stage tags describe location/purpose in an assay, not chemical mechanism.

EDGE TYPES — THREE SEPARATE QUESTIONS

Within a Workflow, retain the existing five Flow kinds:
  material_input: State -> Operation (role attached; not automatic consumption)
  material_output: Operation -> State
  data_input: State -> Operation
  data_output: Operation -> State
  precedes: Operation -> Operation (ordering only, not proof of material transfer)
An instrument is a resource, not a sample State flowing into a reaction. If its
surface is an assay substrate, that material can separately be represented.
No State-to-State shortcut asserts an unexplained transformation. Connections
among components within a State are structural associations, not workflow Flows.

Between Methods/Workflows, retain the definitions already in relationships.py:
  method_development: documented development of the earlier assay architecture.
  component_adoption: documented reuse/adaptation of a specified component.
  mechanistic_precedent: relevant earlier demonstration without proven adoption.
  shared_mechanism: evidenced correspondence, not historical influence.
  shared_purpose: comparable function without requiring a common mechanism.
  comparison: an explicit experimental comparison with its conditions.
Only the first two belong in the historical layer. The catalogue classification
layer is separate from all six. No new historical edge names are needed now.

Each scientific edge is a claim record with: exact implementation endpoints;
relation; component anchors on both sides; before/after change; retained features;
limitation; capability/gain with comparator and context; trade-offs; evidence and
review status. Allow multiple changes, contributors and operation anchors.
An absent predecessor remains an external method/component to research. The
historical graph can be disconnected and have multiple parents; it is not a
phylogenetic tree with a universal distance metric.

MECHANISM AND CHEMICAL NOMENCLATURE

Use ChEBI/PubChem identifiers, molecular structures and qualified end/modification
terms when identity is verified. Formula alone does not specify position,
connectivity, attachment, specificity or compartment. A workflow containing
capture, partitioning or data integration cannot be a balanced reaction equation.
Do not infer an exact oligonucleotide, enzyme formulation, reaction mechanism or
stoichiometry from a commercial reagent name. Missing discriminating chemistry
blocks that mechanistic connection, not every schematic fact in the method.

PIPELINE TO IMPLEMENT USING THE EXISTING CODE

1. Identity and source plan. One scoped method work item with all relevant source
   depictions/text. Reuse the earlier planning work; do not dispatch by source ID.
   Preserve grouped applicability, collisions and source disagreements. Counts of
   source rows and papers are not counts of distinct technologies. A DOI is not a
   unique method key. Do not lowercase away distinctions such as CapSeq/CAP-seq.
2. Extract architecture and method profile. Author uses the typed contract and
   source bundle. Missing evidence creates a targeted acquisition request; it does
   not initiate an unchanged author/reviewer rerun.
3. Validate and independently review. Host validates references, types, vocabulary
   membership, graph incidence, evidence custody and required fields. Reviewer
   checks interpretation, omissions, applicability and consequential routing.
   The reviewer gets property/effect targets, not just a whole-node approval.
4. Publish the scoped architecture with its review level. Preserve original source
   labels and source conflicts. A source-supported sketch is not executable-ready.
5. Trace change. Read the method paper's methodological citation contexts, inspect
   predecessor evidence and locate that method/component in the catalogue. Extract
   author-described differences first; graph alignment checks and localizes them.
   Dates/preprints constrain direction; they do not prove a contribution. Similarity
   retrieval remains unrestricted by date when it is not a historical claim.
6. Retrieve comparison candidates by measurements, operation concepts, substrate
   properties and citations. Align connected groups with boundary constraints and
   permit one-to-many matches. Embeddings/graph matching nominate correspondences;
   independent evidence review admits them. Do not do every possible method pair.

Agent tools required for these phases:
- lookup_method: names, source identity, variants and classification evidence.
- get_workflow / get_operation_group: exact graph, boundaries, provenance, unknowns.
- lookup_operation_concept: definition, arguments, aliases and exclusions.
- request_source: explicit missing claim, source or citation; returns acquisition
  status and a new registered source snapshot rather than an ungrounded answer.
- compare_groups: similarities AND consequential differences; candidate only.
- trace_history: supported contributors and changes with source passages.
Existing graph.py supplies much of graph lookup/group traversal. The shared
concept/method resolver and acquisition handoff are incomplete; tools are not all
available merely because they are listed here.

modifications/compartments, reactions vs catalysts and composite events. Do not
inherit its default that an unspecified sequence entity is unmodified, its
physiological scope, or assumed stoichiometry. Our materials include assay mixtures,
synthetic reporters, beads, droplets and data. A contextual State is not a global
chemical identifier. Docs: https://reactome.org/documentation/data-model

LabOP: adapt typed input/output parameters and separation of object/control flow.

DELIVERY ORDER AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

A. Freeze this small semantic contract and exact examples before further native
   tests. Version terminology; no automatic migration or re-signing old results.
B. Integrate the representation, profile and reviewer targets in the EXISTING
   models/prompts/extraction/graph path. Connect typed retrieval and readable views
   at the same time so structured values cannot disappear in the website.
C. Fresh extraction on Drop-seq, G&T-seq and ClickSeq, on Sherlock Qiu only,
   gpt-6.1-sol/xhigh. Old candidates are comparison baselines, not author inputs.
   These test readout attribution, independent branches and end chemistry.
D. Freeze changes and test held-out PAL-seq and oxBS-seq for mixed readout and
   matched-control structure. They were inspected during the broad audit, so this
   is held out from prompt tuning/native development, NOT an unseen-data claim.
E. Complete a first connected product slice: G&T-seq, scM&T-seq, scBS-seq,
   Smart-seq2, MDA; plus the above three development examples. Primary-reviewed
   links must attach to the retained or changed branch, not just method names.
   Example query: find methylation+RNA at the same cell, trace its DNA-branch
   change, and return the preserved RNA branch and unresolved implementation needs.
F. Only then schedule the full identity-resolved catalogue, incrementally. A new
   method does not require re-extracting all predecessors or comparing all pairs.

Predeclared checks (report denominators, not a single 'accuracy' number):
- Critical fact recovery: supported target, carrier, boundary, branch, chemistry
  and attribution requirements recovered / reference requirements, by category.
- Unsupported assertion rate: unsupported extracted claims / reviewed claims;
  critical unsupported claims block publication for the affected scope.
- Annotation consistency: reference questions answerable from the intended typed
  field, without parsing unrelated prose; evaluate duplicate source depictions.
- Routing correctness: no false pooling of independent libraries, no false
  physical fractions from coexisting species, no action disguised as a State.
- Identity/scheduling: duplicate depictions consolidate; distinct variants and
  same-name collisions remain distinct; unchanged completed work is not relaunched.
- Relation precision and coverage: correct contribution scope on positive cases;
  rejected same-reagent, citation-only, same-purpose and date-only negative cases.
- Usability: graph -> concise method explanation preserves the required facts;
  queries return the same scientific answer under acceptable source grouping.
- Cost: elapsed time, native calls and tokens per unique scoped method and edge.

Software checks can guarantee shape, references and version consistency. They
cannot guarantee entailment, chemistry, novelty or wet-lab success. Automated
scientific review is a gate to evaluate, not an infallible oracle. Unknowns remain
targeted evidence tasks when they matter; routine omitted settings remain outside
the architecture scope. There is no claim that today's draft has passed this plan.
