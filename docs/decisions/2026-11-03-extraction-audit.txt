Contract and alternatives
Keep architecture support, publication identity and a bibliographic mention
distinct. Requiring every secondary bibliography entry to be independently
retrieved would expand the task without helping the depicted architecture.
Silently deleting a secondary mention would discard provenance. Accept it only
as an unassigned listed_not_read annotation with its own exact catalogue quote.
It cannot support method coverage, establish a publication entity, or supply
primary evidence for a correction. Verified architecture claims retain existing
primary-content requirements. No accepted candidate fields or hashes are changed.

Keep current scientific acceptance rules while making their reasons explicit.
Every condition that can withhold acceptance must produce an inspectable target
and explanation; model review and host outcome are both retained. A self-check
reports structural validity separately from the complete scientific outcome.

For missing artifact delivery, inspect structured final-response transport before
changing execution. A successful process exit is insufficient. Provider safety
blocks remain explicit and are never retried through evasive rephrasing.

Tests required
Secondary mentions cannot claim identity/content verification, method membership,
or positive architecture support; two mentions from one catalogue page cannot
collapse into one primary publication. Every host hold has a reason. Existing
accepted records retain their canonical hashes and outcomes. Query misses must
not return the start of an unrelated paper as if a requested passage was found.
Fresh native extraction/review is required before admitting repaired records.

Accepted-record scientific finding
The first-pass MPE-seq poster record accepted a primary correction whose quote
is the article title. Its rationale explicitly acknowledges that the quenching
and reagent correction come from external passages absent from the registry.
The corresponding catalogue record correctly remained research_needed. Exact
quote matching and two reviewers therefore did not establish entailment.
Reject title-only evidence for scientific correction/content support; it can
still establish bibliographic identity. This is a general source-role boundary,
not a keyword rule about MPE-seq. Unrelated body text remains an entailment risk
requiring scientific review; the title guard does not solve that whole problem.
