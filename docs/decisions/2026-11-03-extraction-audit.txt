Contract and alternatives
Keep architecture support, publication identity and a bibliographic mention
distinct. Requiring every secondary bibliography entry to be independently
retrieved would expand the task without helping the depicted architecture.
Silently deleting a secondary mention would discard provenance. Accept it only
as an unassigned listed_not_read annotation with its own exact catalogue quote.
It cannot support method coverage, establish a publication entity, or supply
primary evidence for a correction. Verified architecture claims retain existing
primary-content requirements. No accepted candidate fields or hashes are changed.

Keep current scientific acceptance rules while making their reasons explicit.
Every condition that can withhold acceptance must produce an inspectable target
and explanation; model review and host outcome are both retained. A self-check
reports structural validity separately from the complete scientific outcome.

For missing artifact delivery, inspect structured final-response transport before
changing execution. A successful process exit is insufficient. Provider safety
blocks remain explicit and are never retried through evasive rephrasing.

requiring scientific review; the title guard does not solve that whole problem.

Source-depiction duplication and method identity
The dispatcher schedules all 154 website cards, 285 poster rows and 39 footer
panels separately. It checks unique record IDs, not unique method/version
identity. collection_candidates() supplies reading leads only. The historical
crosswalk is not a deduplication gate in this launcher. Thus Drop-seq received
three extractions, G&T-seq four and ATAC-seq two. Preserving distinct source
depictions was appropriate for provenance; using each as a separate full-method
extraction job caused redundant work. No validated fresh unique-method count
exists. The old 201-entity inventory cannot supply that number retrospectively.

Publication accounting is a different question: 879 distinct registered PubMed
IDs occur in the first-pass registries, with 2,275 record/PMID pairs. One method
can have an originating paper, adaptations, applications and reviews. These are
not 879 methods or 879 full papers read. Highest registered level per PMID is
268 fulltext, 607 abstract and 4 metadata. Repeated PaperMatch objects are not
unique publications and must not be reported as such.

Before another full batch, build an evidence-reviewed method/version work list.
Keep every source occurrence and its provenance. Nominate likely matches using
labels, catalogue links, implementation papers and depicted structure. The
decision must distinguish same scoped implementation, materially different
implementation, and unresolved identity; neither shared name nor shared PMID is
sufficient. The DNA and RNA uses of Nuc-Seq are a negative control. One approved
method/version task should receive all applicable depictions; shared labels must
not force their operations to be identical. Grouped figures can describe several
methods, so source-to-method membership can be many-to-many. Do not destructively
merge existing graph records or claim that this work-list gate is implemented.

This separation has precedent in the distinction between extraction and
canonicalization in EDC (EMNLP 2024):
https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.548/
Our proposed identity gate additionally needs assay-version and figure-scope
evidence; EDC does not establish those genomics-specific judgments for us.
