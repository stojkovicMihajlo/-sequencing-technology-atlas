CHANGE CATEGORIES: PROPOSAL, NOT WIRED AS A REQUIRED ENUM
Latest user asks whether chemical/other technical changes can type connections.
Keep historical attribution separate from technical change categories. A claim
can contain several change records; each change can have several category tags.
Tags describe a supported before/after difference, not the whole method, and do
not establish ancestry, novelty, feasibility, improvement or interchangeability.

Literature checked in primary full-text sections,6November2026:
1. Henderson and Clark1990, Architectural Innovation, ASQ35:9-30,
   https://doi.org/10.2307/2393549
   Author repository abstract: https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/8984/
   distinguishes changes to components from changes to their integration. Adopt
   the distinction as a reasoning aid. Do not map a reaction automatically to a
   whole component or claim this is a sequencing innovation ontology. Direct
   full-PDF access failed; no uninspected implementation or empirical result is
   attributed to it.
2. Visser etal2011, BioAssay Ontology, Results/Main concepts and ontology outline,
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3149580/
   separates format, signal-generating design, detection technology and endpoint;
   describes combined expert-driven and data-driven ontology construction. Adapt
   orthogonal assay-description facets, not drug-screening categories wholesale.
3. Bandrowski etal2016, Ontology for Biomedical Investigations, Results/Classes,
   https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0154556
   distinguishes materials, processes, information, roles and specified input/output.
   Adapt the separation of entities, transformations and information. It is not
   an evolutionary-relation or innovation-type classification.

Provisional categories to evaluate on actual change records:
  chemistry: reaction, reagent/enzyme chemistry, linkage, reactive group or conditions;
  recognition_selection: what binds, is captured, cleaved, enriched or excluded and why;
  physical_organization: material preparation, attachment, compartment or separation;
  workflow_routing: order, placement, branching, joining or pooling;
  information_encoding: what property is recorded, its physical carrier and association;
  instrumentation_readout: device, sensor or physically detected signal;
  computational_decoding: how readout maps to the reported biological quantity.
These intentionally overlap; chemistry is not synonymous with sample preparation.
Performance/cost/resolution are consequences, not technical change categories.
Unclassified supported changes stay in natural language. Extract factual changes
first, then inspect coverage/confusions and revise category definitions across
several families. Do not force an answer, backfill a legacy edge, or add molecular
structure encoding merely to assign a tag. Actual chemical formulas, when supplied
by a source, remain evidence-bearing properties rather than an invented taxonomy.

Recommended design under evaluation: record scoped inheritance assertions and
technical changes separately. First state what the later work demonstrably uses
from which earlier implementation, with actual attribution and earlier evidence.
Then record each consequential before/after difference with States/Operations/
Flows or field anchors, retained features, interfaces, and observed consequences.
One contribution may cover many Operations; one connection may carry several
changes; a single changed Operation can affect the whole measurement. A documented
difference or a reported benefit is not by itself evidence of inheritance.
Keep broad development/adoption labels descriptive until factual records support
their interpretation. Each method also needs a source-backed distinguishing
contribution even when no historical donor is established. No discovered edge
does not prove independent invention. Changing within-workflow granularity must
not change the inferred history merely because Operations were split/grouped.
