HISTORY REVIEW: SUPPORTED OMISSIONS AND RESEARCH LEADS

Observed failure: the completed ATAC review independently accepted13positive
methodological claims but put three future/provenance leads in omitted_candidates.
The current exporter consequently published none. Do not manually delete those
findings or promote the old review's claims. Preserve all original artifacts.

New review contract citation_change_review/2 separates claim findings from typed
coverage findings. A supported omitted contribution requires exact seed and
cited earlier full-paper support. A research lead requests evidence that is not
available or not decisive; it does not assert a new established relationship.
Each coverage finding explicitly names affected existing claim IDs and whether
it blocks them. A blocking finding cannot coexist with acceptance of those
claims. An independent unrepresented contribution marks coverage incomplete,
requests one bounded author revision, and does not by itself invalidate other
individually reviewed statements. No claim of exhaustive historical coverage.

Existing per-claim, per-change, quote, source-level, uncertainty, source-version
and fresh-native-review gates remain. Legacy review1 keeps its old publication
behavior. Graph schema4 keeps the same change/endpoint representation and adds
an explicitly versioned coverage review. Readers check its evidence, IDs and
publication blockers as well as each published relationship. This is not a
relaxation of full-paper or historical-attribution requirements.

Evaluate synthetic negatives for missing/one-sided/abstract-only evidence,
unknown claim IDs, unsupported completeness labels and accepted blockers.
Run a fresh independent review of completed unchanged ATAC research under the
new contract; never rewrite the historical review. Expand to new accepted
methods with the same contract, exact-source reuse and separate reviewed mapping.

Scientific motivation: incomplete literature coverage and falsity of a specified
supported claim are different uncertainties. Both must remain visible. Source
availability does not establish experimental compatibility or novelty. This
change is derived from an observed general failure, not an ATAC answer key.
