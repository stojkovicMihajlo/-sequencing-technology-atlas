Why the visual presentation changed
The full-width, alternating State/Operation graph becomes unreadable when fitted
to one screen. Zoom and fullscreen alone do not solve reading a long sequence.
The default is now a workflow reader: one Operation card, its exact input/output
States, linked producers/consumers, mechanism and information effect. A connection
between two Operations is displayed only through their recorded shared State.
The index is reading navigation; its numbering is not a new execution constraint.
An alternate graph view keeps ELK, uses curved Bezier edges, supports vertical or
horizontal orientation, explicit zoom controls and fullscreen. Compact projection
shows a unary Operation as an edge only when it has precisely one input Flow and
one output Flow and no additional incident dependency. Merges/splits retain nodes.
This is a display projection. Original records are not rewritten.
