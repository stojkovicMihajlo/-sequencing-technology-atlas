experimental-design contribution, described with a matched before/after account.
Chronological difference is primary. Inheritance, method/version identity and
reported performance are separate source-supported judgments. Unchanged reuse,
unknown isolated gain, pure component inheritance and new designs without a
known donor are valid outcomes. A performance comparator is the actual baseline,
not the measured quantity or necessarily the historical donor.
