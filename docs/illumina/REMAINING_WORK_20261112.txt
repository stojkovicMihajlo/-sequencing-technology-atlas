An unextracted historical implementation does not establish that its method is

Before labelling a case a failure, record the exact catalogue match, the expected
coverage and the actual missing output. Unchecked absence is not evidence.

C. Evidence: main papers may be available while supplements, oligo tables or
cited protocol dependencies remain inaccessible. Recover the specific missing
information; narrow unsupported claims where the core diagram is still supported.
Do not manufacture details or call every inaccessible recipe a fatal gap.
Provider restrictions remain stops, not triggers to rephrase or change models.

D. Representation: distinguish actual design changes from reused components and
example specimen differences. Preserve molecular identity, routing and decoding
requirements. Avoid expanding repeated illustrative cells into redundant patterns.

E. Verification: more revision passes do not guarantee truth. A pass requires
source support, consistent material transformations and routing, supported identity
carriers, complete inference inputs and no blocking scientific finding. Format and
source-delivery checks do not prove entailment. Native images require separate
comparison with the approved protocol. New --max-author-passes defaults to6;
previous frozen runs remain unchanged. Correctable unresolved work can continue
from verified saved reviews without rerunning approved methods.

F. Product: preserve direct links to original and corrected records, source
provenance, review findings and generated assets; publish the working local
explorer later. The ideation engine remains a prototype requiring prospective
evaluation; this reconstruction pilot does not establish novel-assay discovery.
