ACTIVE PROMPT AND REVIEW GUIDANCE (8 NOVEMBER)
The six requirements are included in STRUCTURED_INSTRUCTIONS in
src/genomics_discovery/illumina/representation.py under STATE CONTENT REQUIREMENTS.
build_prompt() includes that block once for both author and reviewer using
representation_version=illumina_diagram/1.1, including paper-only extraction.
They require factual statements identifying what exists, the molecule/reporter,
what changed, the next Operation's requirements, what stays together/separate,
and what carries the measurement association. Output is not a Q&A checklist;
genuine missing-evidence questions remain in Unknown. Use existing assertions;
there are no new schema fields or categories.

The adjacent non-exhaustive examples illustrate interpretation, not registered
target evidence or an exhaustive collection of assay types. The separate optional
paper-mode worked-output examples remain unchanged. No example establishes a
fact about a new target without its own source evidence.

Reviewers must identify the unmet requirement, missing fact and exact affected
field, and distinguish omitted supported facts, unavailable evidence, presentation problems
and irrelevant detail using existing targets/actions. Diagram blocks remain the
default abstraction; scientific precision inside a block does not automatically
add nodes. The card reviewer instruction now distinguishes losing independent
processing from representing separate units compactly with explicit organization
and correspondence. This does not authorize automatic graph merging.

These development prompt changes require a fresh fixed-source model comparison
before any claim of improved extraction quality. Local software checks can only
establish prompt delivery and compatibility. Frozen runs must be redeployed in a
new pinned runtime to receive the changes; historical outputs are not rewritten.

A State records WHAT EXISTS at a particular point in the scoped workflow:
an actual sample, fraction, molecular mixture/construct, or dataset. It does not
record the action being performed, the assay's broad application domain, or only
the biological quantity researchers hope to measure.

"Preparation" is ambiguous English: preparing material is an Operation; a
prepared sample is material. Prefer concrete sample/constituent names in State
labels. "Adapter-tagged genomic DNA fragments in a chromatin sample" describes
material; "Tagment chromatin with adapter-loaded Tn5" describes an action.

LEVELS OF DESCRIPTION
1. kind=material|data is only the broad family.
2. Identify the actual object at this boundary: cells, isolated nuclei, lysate,
   a chromatin fraction, extracted RNA, a DNA-probe mixture, bead-bound RNA,
   a sequencing library, sequence reads, alignments, or a named derived result.
   These are examples, not a closed universal list or automatic equivalences.
3. Add source-supported properties needed to understand this object, the next
   transformation, and the measurement it ultimately supports.

MATERIAL STATE QUESTIONS AND EXISTING FIELDS
- What is the actual material/sample? identity at subject=self. Name physical
  material and its supported preparation form; purpose alone is insufficient.
- What consequential constituents/subpopulations are present? components,
  with local subjects for properties. A mixture is not several isolated fractions.
  Do not enumerate every constituent of a cell, buffer or commercial kit.
- How is it organized? physical_context. Distinguish cells/nuclei from lysate,
  solution from immobilized material, intact compartments from mixed contents,
  and separately processed preparations from actual pools, when consequential.
- What molecular features matter? strandedness, topology, terminal_group,
  modification, sequence_segment. Put each on its actual molecular subject.
  Do not copy a template's chemical mark onto its DNA reporter or require a
  full molecular structure/formula for every intermediate.
- What is attached/associated with what? association plus local partner. Binding,
  hybridization, covalent attachment, containment and co-presence are different.
- What identity/measurement association is retained? attribution: unit, actual
  carrier and supported association. Record barcode linkage, sample provenance,
  origin-selected sequence or another relevant carrier without inventing maps.
- How was it obtained and where does it go? Follow the existing producer/input/
  output Flows and Operations. Do not duplicate that graph as another free-text
  history or infer all-to-all molecular derivation from multi-input connectivity.
- What supports these facts and what remains unresolved? evidence and the
  existing exact-target Unknown/Correction records.

DATA STATE QUESTIONS AND EXISTING FIELDS
- What dataset is it? identity and required data_type.
- What observations/fields does it actually contain? data_content.
- What processing has already occurred? Name the actual stage in identity and
  data_type; its producing Operation supplies the process. Raw reads must not
  already assert aligned genomic coordinates introduced by a later mapper.
- How are observations linked to molecules/cells/samples/locations? attribution.
- Which quantities are directly observed and which are inferred? data_content,
  attribution and the source-qualified measurement explanation.
- What supports those claims? evidence and exact-target qualifications.

WHAT IS REQUIRED, AND WHEN
Every State needs a specific identity, source support and correct graph position.
Material identity must distinguish the physical preparation relevant to its use.
Data additionally needs its actual dataset type. Other properties are required
when their omission prevents interpretation of a retained transformation, an
input/output interface, attribution, or a distinguishing method feature. They
are not a demand for every possible chemical or execution detail.

The source scope controls the starting boundary. A method that begins with
purified RNA need not acquire a fictitious cellular starting State. A cell line
used in a demonstrated implementation is context, not automatic evidence of
all-cell applicability. Conversely, keeping general method scope must not erase
the concrete distinction between cells, nuclei, lysate and extracted material.

Missing property means NOT RECORDED. It does not mean absent, not applicable or
unreported by the authors. Available but omitted consequential facts require
correction. Truly missing consequential evidence requires source recovery. An
unnecessary recipe detail does not justify inventing a fact or blocking a sound
schematic. A schema can enforce fields/subjects/references; scientific review
must assess sufficiency and support.

EXAMPLES FROM THE CURRENT EXTRACTIONS
- Drop-seq pooled_beads: RNA hybridized to bead-bound primers, outside broken
  droplets. Barcode/UMI-bearing primers carry the attribution; intact-droplet
  pooling and pooled bead material are different physical contexts.
- ClickSeq s_purified: single-stranded cDNA with a 3-prime azido terminus.
  That feature explains the following click-joining interface. It is not RNA
  merely because the assay measures RNA, and it is not yet a PCR library.
- cP-RNA-seq s1: one gel-purified RNA mixture with different terminal-group
  subpopulations. Their separate chemistry does not establish separate tubes.
- mNET-seq s0_cells: the author mixed general mammalian scope, material identity
  and the purpose of chromatin isolation in the label. The paper explicitly
  reports HeLa cells. Keep the starting material and documented specimen clear.
- irCLIP s0: the author recorded cells-before-lysis in the adjacent Operation
  and quotes while leaving the State identity as generic cellular material.
  The full workflow is coherent; the State is underdescribed in isolation.
