Illumina atlas: inventory numbers

6 November 2026 · Published source library 07 · Source coverage, method identity and diagram sharing

The current library has 273 curated method/implementation entries. Of these, 174 are represented by website cards, 271 by poster rows, and 172 appear in both. These are source-backed catalogue entries; this number does not count completed protocol graphs, historical edges or independently validated inventions.

3. Why 154 cards represent 174 methods

We use the reviewed source-to-method assignments rather than treating every slash-separated name as a new technology. There are 132 cards mapping to one curated entry, 12 mapping to two, 8 mapping to three, 1 mapping to four and 1 mapping to five.

132 × 1 + 12 × 2 + 8 × 3 + 1 × 4 + 1 × 5 = 189
189 − 7 − 8 = 174

The 189 are card-to-entry assignments. Seven assignments refer to family/component records rather than named-method entries. The remaining 182 named-method appearances include eight repeated appearances across cards, leaving 174 distinct named-method entries.

For example, Hi-C/3C-Seq/Capture-C is one card connected to three separate entries and their assigned papers. Drop-ChIP/scChIP-Seq is one entry with aliases. Bru-Seq appears both on its own card and on the grouped GRO/BRIC/Bru/BruChase card, so it is counted once.

Some papers describe more than one method; some methods have several associated publications. Therefore “methods with a paper” and “downloaded papers” are different counts. The card-subset figures exclude papers assigned only to family/component resources.

Recorded exceptions. The MDA/IMS-MDA/MIDAS CMS card is empty: its image and assigned papers are retained, but we do not invent Illumina prose for it. A scBS-seq category-tree reference points to a 404 page; scBS-seq already has a poster-derived method entry. The MPE-seq card has one malformed stored citation URL; the printed 1982 chemistry citation itself is preserved.

The fresh audit retrieved the current All Methods index, three category trees and card content; 616 registered source/article file checks passed. All 174 named card-method entries have paper assignments. This establishes source accounting and file integrity, not full scientific paper review or protocol correctness.

The library also retains 25 separately typed resources: 15 commercial kits, 5 family overviews, 2 reusable components, 1 readout platform, 1 application overview and 1 unresolved source label. They are excluded from the 273 named-method total. The primary method folders are Genome 61, Transcriptome 135 and Epigenome 77; secondary category links do not create duplicate methods.

The earlier provisional 318 folders became 298 curated entries after 21 alias-folder consolidations and one homonym split: 318 − 21 + 1 = 298 = 273 named methods + 25 other resources. This is a separate identity-cleanup calculation.

6. A short update you can use

We’ve organized Illumina’s catalogue into 273 method/implementation entries. The 154 website cards represent 174 named methods, and the three posters add 99 methods not represented by those cards. Each entry keeps its original sources and paper assignments; grouped methods can reference the same diagram. We’ve identified 21 shared-diagram groups involving 53 methods, which gives us a focused starting set for comparing the specific changes between assays. Paper acquisition remains incomplete, and diagram sharing is kept separate from evidence of historical development.
