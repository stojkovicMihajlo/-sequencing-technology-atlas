Module m_specimen: Specimen quantity and selection of a ChIP alternative; members: o_count, o_fix_v1, o_fix_v2, o_fix_v3
  Boundary rationale: Starts at cellular specimens; one target/recipe is selected for a given library. Target-specific low-input demonstrations limit applicability, not universal cell minimums.
Module m_capture: Prepare and enrich target-associated chromatin; members: o_nuclei_v1, o_shear_v1, o_shear_v2, o_shear_v3, o_capture_v1, o_capture_v2, o_capture_v3, o_wash_v1, o_wash_v2, o_wash_v3
  Boundary rationale: Ends at LiCl-washed enriched chromatin on beads. Distinct lysis, sonication, antibody and wash recipes remain alternatives; no hybrid histone/TF recipe is formed.
Module m_tagment: Introduce sequencing-adapter tags before DNA release; members: o_pre_tag_v1, o_tag_v1, o_post_tag_v1, o_pre_tag_v2, o_tag_v2, o_post_tag_v2, o_pre_tag_v3, o_tag_v3, o_post_tag_v3
  Boundary rationale: Begins at enriched bead-bound chromatin and ends at washed tagmented chromatin, preserving branch-specific wash identities around the common tagmentation chemistry.
Module m_release: Release and purify target-attributed tagged DNA; members: o_release_v1, o_release_v2, o_release_v3, o_purify_selected
  Boundary rationale: Deproteinization/crosslink reversal changes the material from chromatin on beads to purified tagged DNA. Versions 1/3 re-extract; version 2 does not inherit that step. Alternative outputs converge without physical pooling.
Module m_library: Analytical aliquot and controlled production enrichment; members: o_aliquot, o_qpcr, o_pcr, o_remove_long, o_recover_library, o_final_qc
  Boundary rationale: Separates measurement material from production material; measured Cq controls production cycle count. Final size-selected library and incompletely specified QC bound sequencing entry.
Module m_reads: Sequence target and independent IgG libraries; members: o_sequence, o_igg_boundary, o_igg_sequence, o_trim, o_align, o_deduplicate, o_igg_process, o_shift
  Boundary rationale: Turns attributed physical libraries into processed target and background data. IgG preparation is a reported boundary with unknown internals, not an invented target-recipe assignment.
Module m_enrichment: Interpret relative genomic enrichment; members: o_tracks, o_peaks_replicate, o_check_replicates, o_merge_replicates, o_peaks_merged, o_frip
  Boundary rationale: Produces normalized genomic tracks, independently/background-called peaks, consistency-gated merged peaks and FRiP. Exploratory structural applications do not become required enrichment protocol steps.
