explicitly from a biological RNA cleavage site. Primer barcode is distinguished
from a molecule identifier. Blunting and second-adapter ligation are combined;
the mechanism still records their order. Operations 7 -> 6.

3c-seq: Retains the paired genomic sequences through the second circular linkage
and biotin-tag construction. The new explanation explicitly distinguishes
amplified copies from independent biological contacts. Same 6 Operations.

3p-seq: Both versions correctly distinguish the short poly(dT) extension from
the final cDNA reporter. The new version explains the RNA release interface and
terminal-A interpretation more directly. Residual adenylates remain in the
mechanism even though the shorter State label omits them. Sequencing is no
longer a separate Operation; its interpretation remains in the final Operation.
Operations 8 -> 7. Do not describe this as newly discovering the RNA/DNA distinction.

4sudrb-seq: Explains that the pulse can overlap the inhibited period and that

5-prime-gro-seq: Better causal explanation of why CIP/TAP processing and adapter

Confirmed BisChIP-seq placement change
OLD: a modification property with subject marked_histones, site histone H3
lysine 27; a separate modification property with subject adapters, with exact
methylated positions unspecified. These address chemical features of molecules.
NEW: defined_other/measurement_target on chromatin.self describes H3K27me3;
defined_other/adapter_chemistry on adapted_dna.self describes adapter methylation.
The scientific distinctions are visible, but a query restricted to modification
properties returns none. The new reviewer explicitly accepted the local
measurement_target property. The schema permits defined_other without requiring
an explanation of why the existing property families cannot represent the fact.
The prompt explains property meanings but does not establish a reliable
precedence/enforcement rule for using an existing family over the fallback.

These four old assertions describe two kinds of chemical fact, with the histone
mark repeated at three States. Matching the old assertion count is not a goal;
consistent placement and a chemically correct subject are the goals.

Follow-up priorities (not yet implemented or live-validated by this audit)
1. Require facts fitting existing property families to be recorded there; retain
   prose for purpose/measurement explanation. Use defined_other for a genuinely
   different assertion, with a reason the existing types do not fit. Review must
   check placement as well as scientific support, including the actual subject.
2. Keep consequential qualifications attached to the claim they qualify. Removing
   source commentary must not remove uncertainty or tested-context limitations.
3. Establish a consistent graph endpoint policy. Interpretation can be explained
   without an extra node, but graph tools must know where that interpretation is
   recorded. Do not infer improved fidelity from fewer nodes or longer prose.
4. Preserve before/after comparisons and evaluate targeted retrieval questions,
   rather than use reviewer acceptance or property counts as the sole quality metric.
