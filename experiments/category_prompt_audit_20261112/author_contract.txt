TASK AND SCOPE
Extract an explanatory assay workflow for the assigned method/version. Explain
what it measures, from what input, and how its physical procedure makes that
measurement possible. Use the matching Illumina description and diagram to set
the decomposition; use the matching primary paper to confirm and explain each
retained operation. Do not reconstruct every experiment or an executable recipe.
Different depictions are sources, not additional methods. A shared diagram does
not make its named methods identical. Respect assigned member/version boundaries.

READ AND CONSTRUCT
Read the catalogue description and inspect the actual attached diagram, including
legends, arrows and molecular symbols. Confirm the operations in the registered
paper's relevant Methods, figures and explanatory text. Check that each passage
describes this implementation. Metadata and abstracts do not establish unreported
chemistry. Record unavailable consequential evidence instead of guessing it.

Start with the diagram's action blocks. Identify the material or data before and
after each block, then connect the actual inputs and outputs. Keep details inside
these blocks first. Add or split a block only to express a supported route,
interface, attribution or method-specific distinction the existing block cannot
express. Matching catalogue prose may supply an omitted preparation or readout;
label its support as textual. Do not add every control, application or analysis
from the paper. Preserve a diagram/paper disagreement and its supported resolution
in Correction; apply the resolved order and chemistry to the graph itself.

FIELD MEANINGS
State names what physically exists, or which dataset exists, at a boundary.
Name the preparation, not its intended use: for example cells, nuclei, lysate,
RNA fragments, a library, raw reads or aligned reads, only as supported. Identify
the actual molecule and consequential properties, including origin, end chemistry,
attachment and compartment when these explain the next operation or measurement.
Distinguish the biological target from a copied or synthetic reporter. A feature
of a template is not automatically physically present on its copy.

Operation.description states what is done and what changes. Mechanism explains
how the supported transformation, selection or inspection works. Name a reagent
or condition when it explains that action or compatibility, rather than listing
routine execution settings. Neither field is a report of the extractor's reading
process. Put diagram interpretation, source comparisons and evidential reasoning
in Evidence.rationale or Correction, not inside the scientific explanation.
Keep relevant scientific qualifications in the explanation itself.

Information_effect explains a specific distinction introduced, preserved or lost:
what remains identifiable, what material or data carries it, and how it supports
the measurement. State the local effect on these inputs, not the assay's overall
goal. Distinguish a recorded feature from the inference or comparison needed to
interpret it. Treatment and control can have different effects. Use null when
there is no distinct supported information effect, rather than inventing one.

Flows connect exact State and Operation IDs. Material/data inputs enter an
Operation; its products leave it. A control-order dependency is not material
transfer. Graph proximity or reachability alone does not establish molecular
derivation. Separate independently routed products. A mixture of molecular types
is not automatically separate fractions. Repeated equivalent processing can stay
compact only when unit/condition identity and input-to-output correspondence
remain explicit. Preserve genuine splitting, pooling and data integration; do
not turn alternative implementations into obligatory consecutive procedures.

STRUCTURED ASSERTIONS
Follow the supplied schema. State.features remains empty. Record a self identity
for each State and a data_type for data States. Put consequential facts into
subject-specific properties, not only inside evidence quotations. Use components
only for constituents/subpopulations requiring distinct assertions. End chemistry
identifies the molecule and 5-prime/3-prime end; associations identify both partners
and whether their relationship is binding, hybridization, covalent linkage or
co-presence. Attribution identifies the unit, carrier and supported association.
Missing properties mean unrecorded, not absent. Do not fill all property families
or invent chemical structures to make the record look complete.

Operation inputs/outputs are State IDs. Input roles describe each participating
subject; multiple roles do not imply chemical consumption. Effects identify the
actual participating inputs and outputs and their change. Output grouping and
resolution describe supported local routing and block structure, not equivalence
between methods. Keep method applicability consistent with each input's history.

EVIDENCE AND COMPLETION
Every factual assertion needs registered support at its actual evidence level.
Text citations contain exact quotations and locations; image claims identify
readable regions. Text cannot make a clipped or illegible feature visually
verified. Distinguish reported, depicted and inferred support; explain inference
in its rationale. Do not turn absence of a description into proof of absence.
Do not fabricate source IDs, biochemical detail, specimen specificity or gains.
Duplicate registrations are not independent corroboration. PaperMatch records
the association and actual inspection level, not verification of every operation.
Unassigned background citations can have methods=[]; positive architecture or
variant support names the supported member. A catalogue citation alone remains
listed_not_read. A needed unregistered source remains an acquisition request.

Retain qualifications that change the measurement's meaning. Keep a consequential
unresolved fact in a targeted Unknown; a routine omitted recipe detail is not a
required gap. Check agreement among State properties, description, mechanism,
information_effect, roles, effects and Flows for every retained branch. A short,
supported explanation is sufficient. Neither valid JSON nor a shared name,
reagent, citation or date establishes scientific correctness, interchangeability
or historical development.
