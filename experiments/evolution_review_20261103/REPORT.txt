Evolution-graph review — started 3 November, completed 4 November 2026

Lessons to carry into the harness
1. The useful connection explains a scoped experimental change. Sharing Tn5 is
   not enough: accessibility-based selection and antibody enrichment make its
   use support different measurements. Record what stays and what changes.
   Historical reuse and the main innovation are separate questions: a valid
   primer-adoption edge does not explain the central novelty of ChIPmentation.
   Keep an evidenced explanation of the method's distinguishing architecture
   even when a historical parent for that change has not been established.
2. The biological target is not necessarily the sequenced molecule. RASL-Seq's
   RNA target is represented by ligated DNA probes. State the carrier and the
   association that makes the readout interpretable; do not copy captions blindly.
3. A historical contribution can be smaller than an Operation. Primer adoption
   inside a broad library-preparation block must not become inheritance of that
   block or whole assay. Allow different contributions between the same methods.
4. Detail sufficiency depends on the question. Two/three-operation diagrams can
   support a scoped relationship, while seven UMI-4C Operations leave the UMI
   mechanism unresolved. Recover the missing distinction, not every recipe detail.
5. A source-qualified architecture is not a general compatibility guarantee.
   A nonblocking schematic gap may block a design claim. Future proposed changes
   must keep experimentally supported properties separate from untested transfers.
6. These lessons support fixed questions/field meanings, not one fixed sequence
   of operations for every technology. They remain candidate harness principles,
   not proven universal laws or demonstrated improvements in model performance.

Decision
Keep the Illumina State–Operation–Flow extractions as source-qualified assay
architectures. Do not restart full protocol extraction or force identical module
boundaries. Build the historical graph through primary-paper citation contexts,
with separate claims anchored to the affected parts of these architectures.
Similarity remains useful for finding analogies; it cannot establish history.

An evolutionary graph here is a directed multigraph, not a single-parent tree.
An implementation can adopt several contributions, have several distinct links
to the same earlier method, or have no established predecessor in our corpus.
Version-scoped historical relations should be temporally coherent; the entire
graph also contains undirected correspondences and therefore is not one DAG.

The principal remaining problems are semantic scope and source coverage, not
whether we can serialize JSON. A valid material route can still lack the detail
needed to explain a particular innovation. Duplicate source depictions are still
not consolidated into reviewed method/version identities. A footer panel is not
automatically an assay. Abstract support is not evidence for unseen protocol
internals. A reviewer accepting a whole State did not establish a separate check
of its display label. The newer rules cannot be retroactively credited to old runs.

Which edges to keep
These six relation names already existed across the two pilots. They are now
defined once in src/genomics_discovery/illumina/relationships.py and supplied to
both prompts. This is our task-specific policy, not a universal ontology.

  method_development    HISTORY. Later implementation develops/adapts the
                        earlier assay architecture; identify retained structure
                        and changes, with actual attribution and two-sided support.
  component_adoption    HISTORY. Later implementation adopts/adapts a specified
                        component/procedure. The surrounding block is not inherited
                        merely because it contains that component.
  mechanistic_precedent PRECEDENT. Earlier work demonstrates a relevant experimental
                        property/mechanism, but adoption is not established.
  comparison            COMPARISON. A paper actually compares methods/results.
                        A reported advantage is not proof of ancestry.
  shared_mechanism      CORRESPONDENCE. Two scoped transformations share a supported
                        mechanism, with substrate and context differences retained.
  shared_purpose        CORRESPONDENCE. Comparable function; mechanism need not match.

Background and no_established_relation remain dispositions, not positive edges.
Within-workflow material_input/output, data_input/output and precedes remain
unchanged. Those Flows must never be traversed as historical contributions.

Do not add a general 'improves' ancestry type. A method may beat a comparator
without developing from it. Improvement belongs in a specific result statement
with metric, comparator and tested conditions. Likewise, 'replaces' can describe
a changed component without establishing who invented it. Multiple design moves
can appear on one contribution claim or in separate claims when evidence differs.

What each relationship records
The existing paired representation already provides the necessary starting point:
  relation + direction       Which claim is made; history is earlier -> later.
  left/right_operation_ids   Exact affected groups in the two immutable workflows.
  shared_description        The scoped common transformation or adopted component.
  difference                Before versus after, including relevant material,
                            compartment, carrier and surrounding-stage differences.
  consequence               Reported capability/gain/tradeoff, or not established.
  rationale                 Why this type and scope follow from the cited context.
  left/right_evidence       Supporting primary passages on both sides.
  unresolved                Qualifications, each independently assessed for whether
                            it blocks this particular relationship.

The citation-following pilot additionally records the nominated reference,
earlier demonstration, limitation addressed, reported gain, transfer limits and
candidate catalogue identities. Those identities remain candidates until reviewed.
It should not invent before-side Operation IDs for an unextracted contributor.

Use the same evidential questions in both stages; do not add another incompatible
universal edge schema now. The remaining production work is a reviewed method/
version registry and a source-located date/citation-identity record. Neither is
implemented by a name hash or by this relationship-query module. Date checking
must distinguish journal, preprint and protocol versions; lack of an earlier
retrieved paper is not proof of historical priority.

Is the current detail sufficient?
For SOME scoped relationships, yes; for arbitrary protocol design, no. Adequacy
must be asked for the proposed claim, not as one blanket score per workflow.

1. Existing ATAC-seq–ChIPmentation pair
   Two and three Operations sufficed for the earlier model-reviewed comparison.
   Shared tagmentation is a mechanism correspondence. Custom Nextera primer
   adoption is a historical contribution inside the two coarse amplification/
   recovery blocks. The earlier pilot did not establish whole-assay descent.
   Paper annotations locate the primer contribution without inventing a primer-
   only Operation. A claim about a changed internal cleanup order would need
   finer evidence/anchoring. Original supplements were not separately inspected.

2. ClickSeq, four Operations in the current catalogue extraction
   The stored architecture identifies azido-terminated cDNA, alkyne-adapter
   coupling and subsequent polymerase read-through, including its limits.
   Those interface properties are more informative than 'adapter ligation'.
   This is enough to pose a comparison with enzymatic adapter attachment. It
   does not establish interchangeability of all polymers, ends or polymerases.

3. cP-RNA-Seq, five Operations
   The stored description distinguishes terminal chemical states through CIP,
   periodate and PNK treatment. The starting modification is removed, while
   selection history survives as differential library competence. Keeping those
   end-group distinctions matters more than expanding every handling step.
   A chemical formula alone would not record that selection history.

4. UMI-4C catalogue record, seven Operations, supported with two Unknowns
   The route is recorded but the physical UMI feature and its point of origin
   remain unresolved. The same gap can be nonblocking for diagram transcription
   and blocking for a molecule-counting comparison or a proposed UMI substitution.
   This is a concrete reason not to treat 'supported' as design-ready.

5. RASL-Seq and scAba-Seq
   RASL-Seq explicitly distinguishes the target RNA from the probe-derived DNA
   reporter and records a source-order discrepancy. scAba-Seq is held because
   the depicted DNA endpoint and T7-IVT interpretation leave a consequential
   product transition unresolved. A shared 'amplification' label cannot settle
   either interface. These are targeted source/interpretation tasks, not a reason
   to reconstruct every experiment from both papers.

Cases 2–5 inspect existing extracted assertions and their recorded limits, not
new independent primary-paper verification. Their exact records are in the pinned
archive and per-record ledger. Only case 1 has the prior paired relationship run.

  anchors into material/information transformations; cited prose alone does not
  establish biochemical compatibility. We do not treat 'causal' relation labels

  dump_line drops context/probabilities. We retain the original context and exact

What changed in this iteration
- One shared relation policy now feeds both existing relationship prompts and
  controls which types the read-only query treats as history.
- Fresh citation nominations must account for every bibliography entry: selected
  with a contribution/reason, or excluded with a reason. The reviewer checks
  exclusions too. This prevents silent omission, but cannot guarantee correct
  scientific selection. Legacy nominations remain readable, not upgraded.
- Accepted relationship support cannot come from a catalogue/metadata record or
  an article-title quotation, even after a positive reviewer verdict. Exact quote
  occurrence remains necessary but insufficient for semantic entailment.
- Both citation tools now use the existing bounded source_excerpt function:
  a query miss returns an empty passage and found=false, not unrelated opening text.
- A read-only query returns the scoped edge, review, exact operation groups,
  boundary states/flows, caveats and source pointers. It reduces unrelated
  provenance while preserving full-registry paths/hashes for further retrieval.
- Shared policy code is pinned with future native run implementations.

  Adey 2010 -> ATAC-seq: tagmentation/library component applied to native chromatin,
                       with accessibility-dependent sampling as the measurement.

What is important specifically for sequencing technologies
The shared instructions now ask five concrete questions for each claimed change:
  1. Which biological quantity, specimen/context and unit are measured?
  2. What physical selection/recording makes it observable, and what molecule is
     actually read? The biological target and sequenced reporter can differ.
  3. What preserves attribution to the original molecule, cell, location or contact
     after handling or pooling? Identify the physical carrier where established.
  4. Which input/output molecular properties and surrounding operations determine
     whether the changed stage connects to the rest of the assay?
  5. What specificity, recovery, bias, resolution or quantitative interpretation
     changes, in which measured comparison, and with what remaining uncertainty?
These are evidence questions, not five compulsory stages or five free-standing
agents. Keep unchanged versus unknown distinct. They are expressed in the existing
claim's scoped description, difference, consequence, evidence and qualifications.
Their inclusion in a prompt is not yet a demonstration of consistent model use.

How an invention agent should use it
First state the desired capability and specimen/context. Retrieve relevant assay
architectures, their documented changes and nearby alternatives. The graph should
help answer 'what changed, where, why, and under which constraints?', not merely
return a predecessor list. Follow precedent/correspondence layers separately when
looking outside a method's historical family.

Your proposed new-node-plus-key-change approach is useful as a PROPOSAL format:
  desired capability and comparison;
  one or more explicit changes to material/information transformations;
  evidence for the required primitive properties;
  untested interfaces and predicted consequences;
  decisive tests and the closest known alternatives found during retrieval.
Changes can have several contributing methods or no existing parent. A combined
gain is not required to be a single edge. An unsupported transfer remains a
hypothesis even if both components separately work. Keep proposed nodes/changes
out of the historical graph until they are demonstrated. Search beyond the atlas
before claiming novelty: a missing edge may reflect missing literature or extraction.
Expand into an execution protocol only after the architectural claim is explicit.

Minimal next native evaluation, before more scale
Use the existing ATAC–ChIPmentation pair as a development control. Choose held-out
relations from different families using primary evidence (e.g. chemistry/terminal
selection, cell identity/pooling, conformation capture). Have reference judgments
for claim type, actual transferred component, direction, necessary qualifications
and missing evidence, without prescribing identical operation counts or labels.
Compare citation-only, citation-context-only and two-sided evidence + workflow
anchors on the SAME candidates; this isolates the value of the graph contract.
Report historical-edge precision, relevant-contribution recall, component-anchor
correctness, preserved qualifications, unsupported interface claims, abstention/
evidence recovery, and cost per accepted claim. Audit a sample of excluded
references; recall over only the nominated list would hide retrieval failures.
Do not substitute exact historical-protocol reproduction for these measurements.

Remaining blockers for a full method graph
1. Reviewed method/version identities before dispatch, preserving each source view.
2. Broader source recovery: the current citation pilot retrieves PubMed/PMC and
   explicitly leaves DOI-only/publisher recovery pending. It cannot yet follow
   every external chemistry or engineering contribution. Do not call that complete.
3. Structured version/date evidence and reference identity checking, including
   preprints where needed, before a production historical ordering claim.
4. Native tests of the new instructions and independently evaluated edge quality
   on more than the one chosen development pair.
5. Targeted recovery of claim-blocking gaps, not blanket re-extraction or arbitrary
   filling of every optional field. A more detailed graph is not automatically
   a more accurate or more useful graph.
