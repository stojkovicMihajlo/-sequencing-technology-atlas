Instruction versus delivery:

Current Illumina reviewer instructions already explicitly require condition-local
information_effect, interpretation dependencies, joint interfaces, source support
and complete fresh review after correction. The host delivers/pins the current
candidate and complete prior candidate/review/index. Main Illumina review gates
already require the exact candidate digest, complete targets and scientific-source
evidence. No missing delivery of these semantics was established by this audit;
the observed first reviews missed claims they were already instructed to assess.

The experimental diagram10 PlanReview lacks a candidate digest despite pinned

That separate concrete binding omission was reported to the parent, which owns
the development fix. No frozen experiment, production code or accepted scientific

for actual Workflow changes; do not add another diff implementation here.
