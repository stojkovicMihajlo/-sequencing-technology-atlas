EVOLUTIONARY PILOT — 12 NOVEMBER

Scope
Earlier method/contribution -> later sequencing method, direction of attributed
use. Multiple contributions and parents are allowed. Shared enzyme, chronology,
citation or a performance comparison alone does not establish inheritance.

Existing record fields (do not introduce a new competing schema)
contributor: the specific earlier contribution actually used and its role.
relation: documented_reuse, mechanistic_precedent, comparison, background,
          or no_established_relation.
change_records: compared_feature; before; after; retained (can be empty);
                workflow_locations; evidence; consequence_and_limits.
Evidence separately supports earlier facts, later facts and the later paper's
attribution. Consequences record actual comparator, context, measured/qualitative
result versus rationale, and whether results concern one change, joint changes
or the whole assay. No invented gain is required for unchanged credited reuse.

Only documented_reuse scoped to the assay workflow is an evolutionary arrow.
Mechanistic precedents and comparisons are useful context, displayed separately.
Refined change categories may be learned later from accepted descriptions.

Endpoints
Connect identified methods when identity is established. If the donor workflow
is absent or unresolved, preserve the named cited contribution/publication with
its identifier as an explicit unextracted endpoint. Do not claim it is an exact
extracted earlier workflow. Exact State/Operation mapping is optional enrichment,
not a prerequisite for a reviewed method-level relationship.

Execution
Freeze accepted workflows independently of the still-running extraction batch.
Traverse the complete later paper, account for bibliography references, acquire
relevant earlier publications, investigate both sides, then independently review.
One bounded author revision followed by fresh review is already implemented.
Missing decisive evidence holds the affected claim; no donor is forced for a
new approach. Record source-tool read receipts, prompts, source hashes, findings,
accepted and withheld links. Full-text delivery does not prove understanding.

Acceptance checks
Can a reader identify which earlier contribution is used, explain the later
implementation and understand what changed or stayed unchanged? Is any claimed
capability/gain supported in its actual comparator and attribution scope? Does
the later paper establish use? Are missing donors/versions and evidence explicit?
Review must detect supported omissions and distinguish those from research leads.

Remaining limitations
The seed citation parser currently requires identity-verified JATS/XML. PDF-only
seed papers are held. Missing supplements and DOI-only/informal references are
not universally recovered. This pilot must report those limits rather than
pretend to provide unrestricted web retrieval. It is not a full-atlas launch.
