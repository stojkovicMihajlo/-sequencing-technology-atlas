Independent novelty audit of the actual ideation demonstration
Date: 2026-11-05
Scope: primary literature and author/vendor protocols; no model rerun, no candidate edits.

ASSESSMENT

These are two untested assay-design hypotheses, not two established inventions.
The broad objective—combining RNA dynamics with protein phenotype—is already
anticipated in published literature. Several individual mechanisms and even the
general strategy of separating an RNA fraction from a protein-associated fraction
are established. I did not find a demonstrated implementation of either exact
proposed pre-chemistry reporter-routing arrangement in this bounded search.
That leaves exact architectural novelty unresolved; it does not establish it.

The most defensible possible contribution is whether this particular early split
improves the fraction of input cells giving reliable, correctly paired RNA-window
and surface-marker results, compared with a strong implementation that omits or
delays that split. No candidate or retrieved source establishes that improvement.
Nor is a dominant reporter-damage or RNA/reporter capture-competition problem
established in these proposed conditions. A different arrangement is not yet a
useful advance. Conversely, established components do not rule out a useful new
architecture if its interfaces and benefit can be demonstrated.

WHAT THE ACTUAL IDEAS ADD

RNA-age candidate, latest available revision:
  4sU-labeled cells receive surface antibody-DNA reporters with a non-poly(A)
  docking sequence. Within each single-cell well, oligo-dT capture retains RNA;
  a paired daughter receives released reporters before RNA alkylation. RNA then
  follows a NASC-style library, and separate RNA/reporter library indexes map back
  to the original well. The revision correctly withdraws the initial custom RNA
  UMI/library claim and acknowledges that fragment counts are not molecule counts.
  Orthogonal docking and early physical fractionation are expressly no longer
  claimed as new. The remaining proposal is the exact pre-IAA split and its
  conditional gain in joint information recovery. That is an appropriately narrow
  claim, but its novelty and usefulness remain to be established.

Physical-selection candidate, initial version only:
  EU-labeled cells receive non-poly(A) surface reporters. The reporter fraction
  is captured and copied separately, while RNA is routed to another daughter for
  off-cell click biotinylation, cell-indexed reverse transcription and pooled
  selection of labeled RNA/cDNA hybrids. The putative difference is moving the
  click step into an isolated low-input RNA fraction after reporter routing.
  EU labeling, click biotinylation, indexing and hybrid selection are established.
  The exact off-cell, low-input, paired-protein implementation is not established
  by those precedents. The candidate also leaves the selected-hybrid-to-library
  dependency incompletely inspected. I have not evaluated a later revision.

These branches differ in RNA labeling/readout—substitution inference versus
physical enrichment—but both center on early separation of surface reporters.
They should not be described as two independently discovered measurement
principles or as evidence of a broadly diverse solution portfolio.

PRIMARY COMPARISONS AND WHAT THEY DO / DO NOT ESTABLISH

[1] CITE-seq, Stoeckius et al., Nature Methods 2017.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4380
    Author manuscript: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5669064/
    Location inspected: opening method description, Fig. 1, Supplementary Fig. 1
    captions, and antibody-oligo/library preparation passages returned by search.
    Establishes same-cell surface-marker and ordinary RNA sequencing. Original
    conjugates use a streptavidin–biotin bridge and a cleavable disulfide, with
    poly(A) oligos captured alongside RNA. It does not distinguish pulse-labeled
    newly synthesized RNA. Thus it supplies components, not an exact solution to
    the full demonstration question. Calling its bridge wholly covalent is wrong.

[2] 10x Genomics, CG000149 Rev C, official demonstrated protocol.
    https://assets.ctfassets.net/an68im79xiti/3CuT1ZcQc4Sd2qZWp2sJA2/d106392c033b911b65ff5d3d7709d07c/CG000149_DemonstratedProtocol_CellSurfaceProteinLabeling_RevC.pdf
    Location: Table 1, printed p.3; capture illustration p.6; conjugation guidance.
    The Feature Barcode oligo has a defined capture sequence instead of a poly(A)
    tail. Consequently, non-poly(A) reporter docking is already standard prior
    art. Its existence does not validate the candidate's custom cleavable H-tail
    reagent, selective partition, or its compatibility with RNA-age chemistry.

[3] CITE-seq authors' protocol updates and FAQ (primary author guidance, not a
    quantified head-to-head test of these candidates).
    https://citeseq.wordpress.com/protocols/
    https://citeseq.wordpress.com/faq/
    Location: October 2017 updates; FAQ sections on conjugation and competition.
    Authors report no observed mRNA/antibody-oligo competition for polyT under
    tested panels, and say a cleavable linker did not help the original bridge
    strategy. They describe later covalent iEDDA conjugation without cleavage.
    This weakens treating shared capture or cleavage as a generally established
    problem/innovation. It does NOT show that a specific pre-IAA routing scheme
    cannot help; that would require its own comparison and a reason for cleavage.

[4] SPARC, Reimegard et al., Communications Biology 2021.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-021-02142-w
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8149646/
    Location: Introduction and Fig. 1a; explanation of PEA readout.
    Oligo-dT magnetic beads retain mRNA, while the protein-containing supernatant
    is removed for proximity extension analysis; RNA undergoes modified
    Smart-seq2. Protein reporters in this study are decoded by real-time PCR.
    This directly precedes early same-cell RNA/protein fractionation. It is not
    nascent-RNA/surface-ADT sequencing, and does not validate H-DNA recovery.

[5] NASC-seq, Hendriks et al., Nature Communications 2019.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-11028-9
    Location: method overview and Discussion; indexed primary passages.
    Combines 4sU labeling/alkylation and single-cell RNA sequencing. The paper
    explicitly identifies UMI incorporation as a future improvement. This is
    relevant support for the revised RNA path, not the current strongest RNA
    baseline and not evidence for the reporter branch.

[6] NASC-seq2, Ramskold et al., Nature Cell Biology 2024.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41556-024-01486-9
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11469958/
    Authors' protocol: https://www.protocols.io/view/nasc-seq2-protocol-cjjiukke.pdf
    Location: Results, "Improved new RNA profiling in single mouse cells";
    Methods, "NASC-seq2 sequencing library preparation"; protocol primer list.
    It miniaturizes alkylation, dilutes DMSO instead of bead purification before
    amplification, includes Smart-seq3 UMI logic, and reports improved sensitivity
    against NASC-seq at matched depth. This is a consequential omitted current
    comparator. Reintroducing capture/washes plus a no-UMI RNA library might cost
    sensitivity or quantification quality. That is a comparison to measure, not
    proof the candidate is worse. This paper alone does not demonstrate the
    proposed surface-protein coassay.

[7] scEU-seq, Battich et al., Science 2020.
    https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax3072
    Accessible primary article copy:
    https://public.websites.umich.edu/~zhanglab/clubPaper/05_22_2020.pdf
    Location: p.1 method summary and Fig. 1A; main article PDF inspected.
    Cells are fixed/permeabilized, EU-RNA is biotinylated in situ, and indexed
    RNA/cDNA hybrids are pooled and separated with streptavidin beads. Labeled
    and unlabeled fractions produce libraries. This establishes the candidate's
    central selection logic; it does not establish moving click chemistry into
    the proposed single-cell RNA daughter after surface-reporter separation.
    The detailed supplement was not independently inspected in this audit.

[8] spinDrop, De Jonghe et al., Nature Communications 2023.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40322-w
    Location: "High-throughput nascent RNA sequencing using spinDrop", Fig. 5A.
    Primary supplement, Supplementary Fig. 2C–F:
    https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/b7df6dea-61f3-4681-ba07-efee28b70e43/content
    Dataset: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM7688449
    This implements scEU-style nascent RNA profiling in droplets. The nascent
    experiment explicitly runs without sorting. Surface-marker-based B-cell
    enrichment is a separate demonstration. Those results cannot be combined
    on paper into a demonstrated same-cell RNA-age/protein coassay. It also uses
    specialized droplet sorting/picoinjection, which is an equipment comparison,
    not automatically an option available to a lab with only a sequencer.

[9] scNT-seq2 / Spatial NT-seq, Nature Neuroscience, published August 2026.
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02420-y
    Location: "Development and benchmarking of scNT-seq2 using in vitro-labeled
    K562 cells"; "In vivo scNT-seq2 library preparation and sequencing".
    Full publisher methods sections were accessible. Optimized second-strand
    synthesis and conversion improve the RNA-side assay. Neither inspected
    workflow is a surface-antibody reporter coassay. The candidate cites an
    earlier preprint; the final methods/benchmark must inform current comparison.

     https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09128-7
     Location: method overview and Discussion; accessible full article.
     Combines biosynthesis labeling with cell identity/function by mass cytometry.
     This already addresses synthesis activity and phenotype, but not
     gene-resolved new-RNA sequencing. The Discussion suggests combining with
     CITE-seq prospectively; that sentence is not a reported CITE implementation.

[11] inCITE-seq, Nature Methods 2021.
     https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01278-1
     https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8532076/
     Location: Discussion paragraph beginning "Future studies can also combine";
     inspected indexed primary text. It proposes future metabolic-labeling
     integration with protein/transcriptome measurement. This is conceptual

[12] NOTE-seq, Lyu et al., Cell Reports Methods, January 2026.

     Location: Discussion; Methods, "Workflow of the NOTE-seq assay" and

     Provides new/pre-existing RNA profiling on 10x. Separate surface-staining

WHAT TO CLAIM NOW

Broad measurement novelty: not established; published authors have already
suggested combining metabolic labeling with antibody/RNA profiling.
Exact implementation overlap: no exact complete match found in this audit;
unresolved, particularly for supplements, unindexed protocols and patents.
Primitive novelty: not the intended contribution; most are known mechanisms.
Architecture novelty: potentially a narrow new arrangement, not proven unique.
Performance novelty: untested; neither proposal has measured improvement.
Validity: mechanistically motivated hypotheses with unresolved interfaces, not
validated protocols. A testable transfer is not automatically invalid because it
has not been run; nor does several individually working parts prove the assembly.

The RNA revision is scientifically more modest than its initial artifact. That
is improvement in claim discipline, not evidence of improved experimental yield.

IMPLICATIONS FOR THE DEMONSTRATION / HARNESS

1. Compare the exact proposed change, not just the full assay's novel name.
   Here the critical contrast is early physical reporter separation versus the
   best feasible matched alternative, with the same reporter chemistry/readout.
2. Include current descendants and author protocol updates in literature review.
   NASC-seq2 and the CITE authors' guidance materially change the comparison.
3. Do not combine different experiments within a paper into a joint assay claim.
   spinDrop is a concrete false-overlap trap. Likewise, an assay listed beside
   another assay in a dataset does not establish same-cell joint measurement.
4. Search alternative readouts allowed by the request. Index-sorting surface
   fluorescence followed by a modern metabolic RNA library is a relevant
   candidate comparator, not a demonstrated combination established here. The
   request does not itself require every modality to be sequenced.
5. Ask whether the proposed interface solves an observed problem. Generic claims
   of reporter damage, capture competition or higher sensitivity cannot become
   the novelty rationale without evidence or an explicit falsifiable hypothesis.
6. Keep conceptual novelty, precise implementation, and useful improvement
   separate. A bounded negative search cannot return "novel=true". The next
   scientific discriminator is a fair comparison, not another rhetorical score.
