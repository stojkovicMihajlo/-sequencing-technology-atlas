RESEARCH AND GRAPH CONTRACT — 5 November 2026

Purpose
Make the genomics inductive bias affect investigation before proposal selection,
not merely the format of a final paragraph. Preserve open assay architectures.
This is a development change; job 51200765 keeps its frozen prompts and schemas.

Decision
Keep the existing coordinator, two independent design experts, literature
investigator, technical reviewer and bounded revision. No extra agent is needed
for this failure mode. Each expert receives four request-specific research
questions: capability, mechanism, interfaces/identity, and alternatives/failure.
These are research responsibilities, not a list of allowed chemistries or methods.
An expert may reframe a question with a recorded reason and investigate beyond it.
Queries and sources remain open, including biology and other engineering fields.

The existing local question bank informs this compression:
  capability: G01/G02/G03/G06 (specimen, direct observation, inference, useful gain)
  mechanism: G04/G12 (required physical property and experimental precedent)
  interfaces: G05/G10/G11/G12 (material state and information carriers across steps)
  challenge: G07/G08/G09 (confounds, alternatives, tradeoffs, discriminating tests)
Source: genomics-discovery/src/genomics_discovery/knowledge/opportunity_questions.json
Four is our practical grouping, not an exhaustive ontology or a proven optimum.
The bank does not prove that all 100+ papers were reviewed for this change.

Each proposal records concise research findings under those four headings,
with exact graph/change targets and supporting references or unresolved questions.
Graph States, Operations, Flows, DesignChanges and their assessment ledger retain
their existing meanings. The reviewer must address the research findings as well
as the graph. Schema completeness cannot establish sufficient research or entailment.

Versioning
New author outputs use proposed_assay/1.1 and new run configurations use
ideation_run/2. Legacy proposed_assay/1.0 remains readable without injecting new
default fields or changing its canonical digest. Native output bytes and old
review bindings remain preserved. Archived contracts use their original schema.

Connection correction
One DesignChange can describe several prior and proposed elements. It does not
assert that every prior element independently maps to every proposed element.
Export a proposed_change node with earlier_basis and proposed_effect edges.
Flow targets remain edge anchors in the change record, with an explicit flow
reference node for node-edge traversal. Never assert historical development,
biochemical equivalence or tested compatibility from this proposal overlay.
