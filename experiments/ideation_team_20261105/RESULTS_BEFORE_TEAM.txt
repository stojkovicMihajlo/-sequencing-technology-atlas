Scientific issues worth keeping, and their limits
1. Closest-prior investigation mattered. The iteration review reports a primary
   targeted-SLAMseq protocol already choosing T-rich targets/read positions. That
   challenges the draft's generic uridine-aware novelty rationale without proving
   the entire proposed surface-plus-RNA assay is already demonstrated. It requires
   claim narrowing and a competent comparator, not automatic rejection or a claim
   of novelty from the absence of an exact match. No fresh source verification is
   claimed by this audit.
2. Attribution must survive before the barcode becomes covalent. Pooled templates
   are exposed to added chemistry while original-cell association depends on bead
   hybridization. A later correct barcode sequence cannot rule out prior exchange.
   The reviewer identifies this as a testable context transfer, not an invented
   intrinsic primitive or demonstrated incompatibility. This distinction is sound.
3. A comparison after an intermediate split only measures conditional performance.
   The proposed paired cDNA-finishing experiment cannot establish losses that
   occurred before that cDNA existed. Full-measurement benefit needs the upstream
   recovery denominator as well as final library efficiency. This is a general
   experimental-design issue, not specific to one labeling chemistry.
4. A method name is not a compatible construct. The review finds that targeted
   amplification is being transferred to a differently prepared substrate with
   different handles. The general PCR mechanism is described, but actual strand,
   primer, barcode and adapter geometry remains to be supplied. This does not show
   PCR is impossible; it limits what this draft currently specifies.
5. Measured conversions do not uniquely identify all biological rates/fractions.
   Incorporation, recovery, error and coverage can remain confounded. The candidate
   appropriately permits model-conditional output or non-identifiability; useful
   uncertainty statements must survive export and demonstration.
6. The record still contains annotation leakage: me_sequence_adt repeats the RNA
   R2-window instructions inside the antibody-library sequencing Operation. This
   is a concrete minor role/copying inconsistency, not a fatal mechanistic flaw.
   Complete target enumeration did not ensure all field interpretations were clean.

What retain_for_testing should mean here
Retain a sufficiently specified, precedented and falsifiable proposal for further
assessment while preserving testable transfer risks. It does not mean completed
chemistry validation, novelty, benefit, an executable laboratory protocol, or a
cleaned final graph. Missing empirical yield can remain an explicit research risk;
an undefined essential signal/identity handoff cannot be excused merely by proposing
to test it. Repairable written errors and undisclosed close overlap should be
corrected in the candidate before presenting it as the revised design.

The iteration final review is useful, but it did not perform that latter repair.
Its retained artifact still carries the original change description and comparator.
Show the review with it and label outstanding design corrections explicitly. The
correct conclusion is “a conditional research concept survived review,” not “the
system produced a reliable new technology.”

Does the expanded architecture cover these classes?
Yes, with limits. Independent designers preserve initial alternatives; peer comments
can expose duplicated reasoning and cross-design assumptions. The chemistry/interface
specialist is explicitly instructed to inspect primitive properties and handoffs.
The measurement/benefit specialist covers identifiability, controls and comparators.
The literature stage checks each exact proposal; synthesis must preserve or explicitly
resolve every specialist objection. This is targeted to observed failure modes,
not a reason to add more unconstrained agents or demand consensus.

Remaining general gaps to watch in the next run:
- team_workflow.py revises a candidate when disposition is revise or when it receives
  peer comments. A retain/hold with no peer comments can still bypass repair of a
  known written error or novelty-attribution/comparator defect. Empirical tests and
  immediately actionable document corrections need visibly different dispositions.
- Only synthesis performs the full graph-target review; specialists inspect their
  relevant paths. This controls duplicate work but keeps synthesis a latency and
  semantic-quality bottleneck. A longer checked-target list is not quality evidence.
- Specialist reports refer to initial versions. Final synthesis checks revisions;
  the system must not describe initial specialist agreement as fresh approval of
  new mechanisms introduced during revision. Current code correctly preserves
  that scope; actual final decisions still need inspection.
- Computable design assertions can sometimes be checked before wet-lab work. When
  sequences and read layouts are available, a small actual construct/geometry check
  is stronger than another prose assurance or a future-test promise. Do not require
  an invented panel where inputs are unavailable; record the missing input instead.
- Four roles, distinct mandate text and a peer round do not guarantee four distinct
  mechanisms or useful discussion. Report surviving branches, shared design moves,
  actual comments/responses and remaining disagreements. Do not count role names as
  scientific diversity or missing candidates as rejected ideas.
