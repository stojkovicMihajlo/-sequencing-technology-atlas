Issues and decisions

01. Oversized correction delivery — repaired; one live case validated
SPLiT-seq's original correction prompt was 1,674,497 characters. The review had
441 checks, of which three requested changes to one barcode-history assertion.
Inlining the complete candidate and repeated evidence caused the transport failure.
The earlier repair uses pinned candidate/review files and a complete addressable
feedback index; the replay prompt was 31,630 characters. No findings were dropped.
Fresh correction job 51210466 completed with a fresh supported review: 19
Operations, 40 States, 64 Flows. See transport_diagnosis.json/split_correction.
This establishes that the repaired handoff worked for this case, not that every
scientific field is independently correct. Keep bounded readers and exact pins.

02. Citation ranges lose relevant references — fixed in this audit
Some JATS paragraphs print 7–11 but link only endpoints 7 and 11. The old parser
missed 8–10, even when a reviewer correctly requested the contribution in one of
those papers. The agent could not satisfy the citation-context gate with that
bundle. This is a parser failure, not a reason to relax historical evidence.
illumina/citations.py expands only an unambiguous printed numeric range whose
endpoints and every interior reference have unique explicit bibliography labels.
It records the derivation separately from explicit XML links. No reference-ID
number guessing, comma expansion, or inference of ancestry is allowed.
Source-derived replay recovers seven middle-reference contexts in Bru-seq,
THS-seq, Smart-seq and PRO-seq. See citation_parser_replay.json and its fixture.
Independent review identified a nested-block false-expansion case; a negative
regression and structural-boundary guard are part of the repair.
Remaining limitation: this is paragraph-based JATS support, not complete handling
of every citation style, tables, HTML or PDF bibliographies.

03. Missing documents are sent to a correction author — recovery still missing
The existing correction deliberately has a fixed evidence bundle. Its prompt
already says so. Rewriting cannot obtain a missing supplement or delegated
protocol. MARS-seq's pooled cellular-identity question and original HITS-CLIP's
protocol/version questions require different evidence from routine wording fixes.
Required change: a review issue identifies the scientific question, affected
claim/record and document needed. The host routes it to existing source acquisition,
checks document identity and lawful access, registers a new immutable bundle, and
requests a fresh affected extraction/review. A failure to obtain it stays explicit.
Do not silently replace an original paper with a later implementation.
Do not change the existing exact-bundle correction seed to accept arbitrary bytes.

04. JATS-only history seeds — genuine implementation restriction
history_stage._paper permits a registered PDF for pair evidence, but initiating
citation tracing requires JATS/XML. Thus a readable PDF does not make the history
stage ready. This is not evidence that the method has no predecessor.
Required change: an alternative citation-index adapter must preserve source file,
page/paragraph locator, reference identity and resolution uncertainty. PDF/HTML
support needs validation of both bibliography matching and callout location.
An LLM-generated list of plausible predecessor papers is not an equivalent input.

05. Submitted-claim checking misses absent information — enforcement unvalidated
An evidence quotation may contain a fact that the graph never asserts. The prior
five-case audit confirmed this for MARS-seq's cleanup before pooling; comparable
questions arise for capture handles and readout allocation. Counting reviewed
States/Operations does not measure coverage of the source.
Current prompts ALREADY ask for source-to-graph coverage, assertions outside
quotations, and scoped interface/carrier details. Do not call these absent rules.
Required change: make that check observable with a compact source-grounded list
of consequential facts and a disposition for each: asserted at a record/field,
outside the fixed scope with a reason, or unresolved with an evidence request.
No fixed fact count and no required new Operation for each fact. A composite
Operation can contain the necessary assertion. Presence of the list still does
not prove the list itself is complete; evaluate it against curated test cases.

06. Equivalence can hide the same omission on both sides — evaluation blind spot
28 comparison findings have no targets on either side. These are investigation
leads, not 28 independently established shared omissions. Targeted checks found:
- CITE-seq: unbound-antibody removal before encapsulation is in the supplied
  primary text but absent from the asserted graph. It can be part of the binding
  operation, rather than requiring a separate wash node.
- DroNc-seq: the smaller bead/channel compatibility requirement matters to the
  smaller-droplet arrangement. A generic statement about miniaturization omits it.
- MULTI-seq: additive-primer amplification is described in the source, but there
  is a stated exception in a proof-of-concept nuclei experiment. Recording it as
  universally mandatory would be another error.
- Microfluidic scATAC: Tn5-complex release is a consequential interface mechanism.
  A condition tested in an explanatory panel must not be promoted automatically
  to the final protocol's recipe.
- Smart-seq2: the catalogue lists more changes than LNA alone. Its mention of
  eliminated purification does not license deleting every cleanup from the graph.
Other evaluator suggestions remain unadjudicated; audit.json preserves them.
Required test: score against scoped source facts, including common omissions,
not merely whether a candidate resembles or improves on the old extraction.

07. Scope is allowed to drift — tighter boundary, flexible internal grouping
The same named assay/version can have different starts, endpoints and branches.
icSHAPE is a clear example: an Illumina strip ends at cDNA; complete reactivity
measurement also needs controls and downstream processing. Those are different
coverage targets. Conversely, preparation of the RT substrate can clarify an
interface inside the depicted route without expanding to a full assay.
Current author/review/evaluation prompts distinguish these cases, but the pilot
does not provide a controlled test of their revised wording.
Required change: freeze assignment scope before scoring: source depiction,
method/version, start, endpoint and required branches. Keep an explicit extension
separate from that baseline. Do not let either author or evaluator move the
boundary to justify a preferred output. A grouped schematic is not proof that
all listed technologies have identical implementations.

08. Source modality can be misattributed — confirmed accepted-output defect
CEL-seq's candidate attributed full paired-strand detail to a diagram that did
not show it. RT was supported by other prose; the visual attribution was wrong.
The previous audit inspected the actual registered image. A valid source ID and
an accurate quotation elsewhere do not validate an assertion about an image.
Modality separation is now explicit in prompts; independent detection performance
is untested. Add a reviewer test with a true fact attributed to the wrong source
modality, alongside a properly attributed positive control.

09. Corrections must update topology and state histories, not just prose
The BruChase comparison showed improvement when corrected ordering was expressed
in the actual graph rather than only a correction note. SPLiT-seq's reviewer
caught a barcode-history claim that placed an identifier before its introduction.
Keep the existing instruction to update affected Operations, State histories and
Flows together. Test that swapping order or introducing a carrier too early is
detected even when the narrative and citations look plausible. JSON validity
and acyclicity cannot prove the scientific order.

10. Global omission holds conflate incomplete coverage and invalid claims
Citation publication currently applies omitted_candidates to every accepted claim
before several other eligibility gates run. There are 178 such hold rows:
39 component-adoption, eight method-development, 55 mechanistic-precedent,
50 comparison, 15 no-established-relation and 11 background claims.
Thus 152 non-background/non-negative claims received this reason, but only 47 are
historical types. They are NOT 152 independently publishable historical edges.
Across the 178 rows, 17 historical claims also concern something outside the seed
workflow, 16 lack citation context and three lack predecessor evidence. Flags
can overlap; other semantic/source checks may fail too.
Similarly, three pair overlays are withheld as a whole when one missing route
needs finer extraction. The iCLIP/eCLIP examples concern a control-route distinction;
the existing comparisons are not all automatically invalidated by that omission.
Required change: a fresh review identifies affected claim IDs and whether the
issue invalidates them, leaves the neighborhood incomplete, or invalidates the
whole analysis. Preserve a global-invalidity option and ALL other admission gates.
Do not reinterpret old prose as authorization to release old held relationships.
Current prompt says adequacy is claim-specific; publisher/schema cannot yet fully
express that distinction. This is an orchestration mismatch, not just wording.

11. Revision stopping must depend on the issue, not endless rewriting
Prior-critique delivery and instructions to account for it are already present.
Current bounded cycles do not deterministically establish resolution of every
scientific objection. No particular round count is established as optimal.
For each outstanding issue distinguish: repair from current evidence, acquire
evidence, clarify scope, reject the claim, or stop for execution/policy failure.
Stop when the claim is resolved/withdrawn, necessary evidence remains unavailable,
or a revision changes neither the relevant evidence nor the unresolved assertion.
A maximum round count bounds expense; reaching it never implies acceptance.
An independent reviewer should judge resolution; the author cannot self-approve.
Multi-round ideation-author revision remains separate pending work.

12. Provider failure, missing acknowledgement and scientific abstention differ
transport_diagnosis.json pins the exact terminal events and result files.
SHAPE-MaP citation nomination ended in a provider biological-risk rejection.
It remains stopped; no prompt rephrasing or alternate provider was used to bypass
that decision. It must not be counted as a missing-source or malformed-JSON case.
Quartz-seq2, Bru-seq and ChIPmentation evaluations instead ended with exit code 0,
turn.completed and written artifacts, but an empty required final acknowledgement.
The existing transport check rejected them. Explicit acknowledgement instructions
are already in the current evaluator; their effectiveness needs a fresh run.
Do not weaken terminal-success, custody or validation gates to inflate completion.

13. Retrieval duplication and failures inflate work and blur evidence quality
The citation bundles contain 526 non-seed source registrations: 286 full text,
221 abstracts, seven metadata-only, 12 unavailable. These are registrations,
not distinct papers or independent supporting observations.
The audit finds duplicate candidates in all 50 extraction bundles using matching
identifier/title/text. This is not authority to merge methods or different versions.
The existing search tool already consolidates exact duplicate renditions while
preserving association IDs. Upstream acquisition consolidation remains separate.
67 registration records contain HTTP 400 and three contain HTTP 429 diagnostics;
these are not necessarily unique requests. Bad-request parsing/rate limiting
needs a different response from an absent or inaccessible paper.
Required change: classify retrieval failures, retain response diagnostics safely,
reuse verified cached originals, and retry only appropriate transient failures.
Source presence, content readability and applicability to this implementation
are three different checks.

14. External contributions should not be forced into atlas method nodes
The pair candidate log contains 403 unbound nominations: 216 outside the extracted
workflow, 187 without an extracted implementation for the cited primary paper.
Many citations legitimately concern analysis, chemistry or general components.
Keep the scope/disposition rather than manufacturing an Illumina assay match.
Publication identity is candidate retrieval; a fresh comparison decides whether
the same implementation and the claimed historical contribution are supported.
Likewise, earlier publication alone is not demonstrated inheritance or priority.

15. Review volume and latency — remove duplication, not meaningful branches
Recorded extraction attempts total about 13.79 worker-hours; citation/pair work
about 40.72 worker-hours. These sums are not elapsed wall time or price estimates.
History is a substantial cost center, not a negligible final placement step.
PRO-seq's separate nucleotide routes and PIP-seq's footprint/control routes have
meaningful ordering differences. Collapsing them solely to reduce node count
would lose scientific content. Reuse addressable evidence and common descriptions,
but retain distinct branch identity and actual transitions.
Prioritize investigating in-scope methodological contributions; retain cheap
documented dispositions for background/comparison citations. Never impose a
quota requiring a positive ancestor edge for every method.

What to keep
Illumina-level architecture as the baseline; paper-supported clarifications with
provenance; local Operation/State/Flow instances; separate historical and similarity
relationships; exact source/graph pins; independent review sessions; retained
unknowns; original artifacts; rejection of unsupported links. More agents, fixed
universal module lists, or detailed chemistry for every node do not follow from
this audit. Nor does it establish that current accepted graphs are all wrong.

Minimal next release and its tests
1. Ship the citation parser regression repair and retain all later relation gates.
2. Wire one explicit recovery handoff into existing acquisition/registration tools.
   Test a recoverable supplement, an unavailable document, a wrong-version match
   and a bundle change that must invalidate the old review. Recovering a document
   is not itself scientific acceptance.
3. Make source-fact coverage and review-issue disposition visible at fixed scope.
   Test omitted-but-quoted facts, false visual attribution, wrong ordering and a
   legitimate out-of-scope detail. Use the existing reviewer, not a new permanent
   specialist team by default.
4. Add claim-specific impact to omission/refinement review before relaxing global
   holds. Test unrelated omissions, genuinely dependent claims, global identity
   failure and every existing downstream gate. Old reviews remain held.
5. Freeze source bundle, assignment and model configuration for old/new harness
   comparisons. Use MARS-seq/CEL-seq and one held history pair for development,
   then reserve an unaffected family for evaluation. This is a proposed small
   validation set, not a submitted batch or proof of generalization.

Report separate measures: execution completion; supported consequential assertions;
unsupported assertions; missing required source facts; correctly retained unknowns;
reviewer detection of deliberately seeded errors; and historical claims whose
before/after change can be reconstructed from the evidence. Do not combine these
into a single uncalibrated quality number. Exact wording/node count is not a target.
Human/adjudicated source checks remain necessary to calibrate scientific scoring.

Implementation references and why they matter
- NISO JATS 1.4 rid specification:
  https://jats.nlm.nih.gov/archiving/tag-library/1.4/attribute/rid.html
  rid links existing IDs; it does not define a numeric interval. Our printed-range
  derivation therefore needs explicit bibliography labels and separate provenance.
- GROBID 0.8.2, ReferenceMarkerMatcher.getNumberedLabels/matchNumberedCitation:
  https://github.com/grobidOrg/grobid/blob/0.8.2/grobid-core/src/main/java/org/grobid/core/utilities/matching/ReferenceMarkerMatcher.java
  It expands numeric callouts and matches bibliography labels, retaining unmatched
  cases. We inspected this related implementation; no code/dependency was copied.
  Our JATS input already supplies endpoint IDs, so we additionally require their
  agreement with unique labels. We do not adopt its arbitrary MAX_RANGE=20 cap;
  our bound follows the supplied bibliography and complete label coverage.
- OpenScholar, pinned 0e9b8fb912273d3dae39e593da86e4f6d3bf8de1,
  src/open_scholar.py, get_feedback and edit_with_feedback_retrieval:
  https://github.com/AkariAsai/OpenScholar/blob/0e9b8fb912273d3dae39e593da86e4f6d3bf8de1/src/open_scholar.py
  Its separation of feedback-driven retrieval from editing is the relevant
  precedent. For us, retrieved documents also need identity/custody registration
  and a new evidence-bound review. This recovery integration is still proposed.
- PaperQA2, tools at c692d0dfeac4a0296599f7fe21a253a58b7ac12a:
  https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa/blob/c692d0dfeac4a0296599f7fe21a253a58b7ac12a/src/paperqa/agents/tools.py
  Search, evidence gathering and answer generation are separate tools. That
  supports explicit source/evidence transitions; it does not establish assay
  compatibility or historical ancestry. PaperQA2 is not integrated into this pilot.
