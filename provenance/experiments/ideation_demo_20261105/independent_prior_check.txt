INDEPENDENT PRIOR-ART AND COMPATIBILITY CHECK — 5 November 2026

Evaluation-only memo. Do not put this file in the native agents' prompts, source
registries, request, or runtime context. Prepared before inspecting their output.
This is a bounded prior check, not a systematic review or novelty certificate.

Approved question as communicated to the evaluator
------------------------------------------------
Propose a sequencing technology measuring newly synthesized RNA and cell-surface
protein identity in the same cell, using existing sequencing equipment.

Interpretation to check against the actual request
------------------------------------------------
The RNA measurement should identify transcripts made during a labeling window,
not merely overall RNA abundance, inferred splicing velocity, or a single global
RNA-synthesis scalar. The protein channel can measure a defined antibody panel;
it must not quietly claim an unbiased complete surface proteome. Same-cell linkage
must be experimentally encoded, not assigned solely through computational matching
of different cells. Existing sequencer compatibility does not imply that every
sample-preparation device, reagent or chemistry is already in the laboratory.

What the search establishes so far
---------------------------------
CITE-seq establishes RNA plus surface-protein readout; scNT-seq establishes new/old
RNA readout per cell. Neither cited foundational paper, by itself, demonstrates
the full requested combination. SOM3B already combines biosynthesis measurements
with immunophenotype at single-cell resolution using mass cytometry, and its
discussion explicitly suggests a future CITE-seq combination. Therefore the broad
idea of coupling biosynthesis and protein phenotype is not a new conceptual gap.
The precise sequencing implementation, its molecular linkage and its useful gain
still require comparison with the actual candidate and a wider prior-art search.

No full same-cell transcript-resolved nascent-RNA plus surface-protein sequencing
implementation was established in this bounded search. That is a search limitation,
not evidence that none exists. Do not score the native system as novel just because
it proposes combining two familiar assay names.

Six primary references and their actual scope
--------------------------------------------

1. Stoeckius et al. CITE-seq (2017).
   Large-scale simultaneous measurement of epitopes and transcriptomes in single cells.
   DOI: 10.1038/nmeth.4380
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5669064/
   https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4380
   Locator: main method description; Figure 1; Online Methods, antibody conjugation
   and antibody-oligo sequences.

   Demonstrated: antibody-derived DNA tags and cellular mRNA captured through an
   existing single-cell library workflow, sharing a cell barcode; ADT and cDNA
   libraries can be separated and sequenced. Drop-seq and 10x implementations are
   reported. The original conjugation uses a reducible disulfide-containing link.
   Not demonstrated here: metabolic RNA age measurement, compatibility of ADTs with
   any added conversion chemistry, or universal compatibility of every antibody/tag
   formulation. Reductive tag release is therefore a routing question to inspect,
   not automatically a failure: release inside a correctly indexed compartment can
   be intended. Releasing tags before cell attribution would require a different
   justification. Protein reads identify antibody-defined targets; they are not
   direct protein sequencing or a complete proteome.

2. Qiu et al. scNT-seq (2020).
   Massively parallel and time-resolved RNA sequencing in single cells with scNT-seq.
   DOI: 10.1038/s41592-020-0935-4
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8103797/
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-0935-4
   Locator: method overview and Discussion; Methods, chemical conversion on beads
   and SLAM-Seq reaction on barcoded beads.

   Demonstrated: metabolic labeling, droplet capture and conversion on pooled
   barcoded beads support new/pre-existing RNA measurements within the same cell.
   The Methods also report difficulty amplifying cDNA under the tested standard
   on-bead IAA condition, motivating a modified condition in that comparison.
   Not demonstrated here: joint ADT recovery or a generally interchangeable choice
   of conversion reactions. Evaluate when the cell association becomes secure,
   whether it is initially hybridization or a copied barcode, and what happens
   before reverse transcription. Do not infer that pooling is intrinsically wrong:
   the reported bead workflow provides a precedent, while newly added tag behavior
   still needs support. Counts carrying conversion evidence are observations;
   unlabeled/new fractions and kinetic rates require inference and controls.

3. Kimmey et al. SOM3B (2019).
   Parallel analysis of tri-molecular biosynthesis with cell identity and function
   in single cells. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-019-09128-7
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09128-7
   Locator: Discussion, final paragraphs; Methods, Antibody staining and data acquisition.

   Demonstrated: single-cell biosynthesis activity for DNA, RNA and protein together
   with antibody-based cellular phenotype, using mass cytometry; experiments include
   cell lines and primary human samples. The authors explicitly discuss CITE-seq
   integration as a possible extension rather than an experiment completed there.
   Not demonstrated: gene-resolved nascent RNA sequencing plus ADTs in that paper.
   Evaluation significance: reject a blanket claim that connecting biosynthesis
   and phenotype was previously unimaginable; equally, do not label a sequencing
   design directly duplicated by this paper unless its stated measurement is only
   global biosynthesis. Distinguish an earlier conceptual suggestion, a component
   precedent, and a demonstrated complete implementation.

4. Zhang et al. metabolic-labeling benchmark (2025).
   Benchmarking metabolic RNA labeling techniques for high-throughput single-cell
   RNA sequencing. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-61375-z
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61375-z
   Locator: Figure 1; Results, Experimental design and computational quality control;
   comparison of ten conversion conditions and Discussion.

   Demonstrated: direct comparison of conversion conditions in a common Drop-seq
   setting, initially using ZF4 cells. Conversion timing and chemistry affect
   substitution rate, cDNA integrity and recovered complexity. This is evidence
   that naming a known conversion does not establish interface equivalence.
   Not demonstrated: those conditions preserve every antibody, linker, ADT capture
   sequence or added library channel. A candidate should identify what is exposed
   to conversion, what is separated beforehand, and what still requires testing.
   Do not extrapolate the reported condition ranking universally to another cell
   type or platform. It supports comparing conversion yield AND RNA recovery, not
   declaring success from a high conversion fraction alone.

5. Mule, Martins and Tsang. dsb (2022).
   Normalizing and denoising protein expression data from droplet-based single
   cell profiling. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-022-29356-8
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-29356-8
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9018908/
   Locator: Abstract, experimental analysis of ambient antibody background, Methods.

   Demonstrated: substantial ADT noise from unbound antibodies and cell/droplet
   technical variation; empty droplets and isotype/background signals provide
   useful controls in the studied multimodal datasets.
   Not demonstrated: a correction can rescue arbitrary physical tag exchange,
   chemically destroyed tags or all new chemistry-related artifacts. Evaluate
   whether the proposed readout retains background observations and whether its
   claim is calibrated protein-marker evidence rather than exact molecule counts.
   Lack of an explicit choice of dsb is not a failure; lack of any treatment of
   background and misattribution can be a material scientific omission.

6. Burger et al. 4sU perturbation (2013).
   4-thiouridine inhibits rRNA synthesis and causes a nucleolar stress response.
   DOI: 10.4161/rna.26214
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3866244/
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24025460/
   Locator: Abstract; Results on rRNA production/processing, NPM1, p53 and proliferation.

   Demonstrated: dose-dependent interference with rRNA biology and stress responses
   under the tested cellular exposure conditions. This refutes an unconditional
   assertion that metabolic labeling leaves cell state unchanged.
   Not demonstrated: every short pulse, every dose or every cell type is unusable.
   Evaluate whether a candidate recognizes labeling as a possible perturbation and
   proposes an appropriate matched control/calibration. Do not turn this paper into
   a universal numerical exclusion rule or assume it proves a specific surface
   epitope is damaged. That would exceed the demonstrated scope.

Independent assessment questions for the eventual output
-------------------------------------------------------
These are evaluator questions, not additional hidden generation requirements.

A. Does it satisfy the requested measurement? Identify both sequence channels,
   their actual information carriers and the exact point that assigns common cell
   identity. Does it distinguish gene-resolved new RNA from global synthesis?
B. Is the proposed advance concrete? Compare it with the nearest implementations,
   not a straw-man baseline. Record a residual difference and expected gain; an
   untested combination may be useful but is not thereby demonstrated novel.
C. Can a reader follow material/data routes? Check retention of RNA, antibody tags,
   bead association, any separated libraries, and the connection to cell barcodes.
D. Which precise required properties have precedent? For a transfer, determine
   substrate, chemical exposure, capture and amplification assumptions. A familiar
   component name does not close the interface claim.
E. What is inferred? Absence of a conversion does not automatically prove old RNA.
   Calibration/background, incomplete labeling and sequence coverage should be
   addressed at the level needed for the claim, without demanding every recipe detail.
F. Are uncertainty and evidence honest? An inaccessible supplement is not a negative
   experiment; a future-work paragraph is not an achieved result. Test proposals are
   not measured outcomes. Existing sequencer use is not evidence that the whole assay
   is inexpensive or requires no other instrumentation.

Do not require the model to reproduce a preferred architecture. Record whether its
different architecture meets the same measurement/attribution requirements, what
its unsupported assumptions are, and what experiment would discriminate success
from its stated failure mode. These judgments remain independent of graph syntax.

Search and access record
------------------------
Web searches used combinations of: newly synthesized RNA + CITE-seq; 4sU + CITE-seq;
metabolic labeling + surface proteins/antibody-derived tags; nascent RNA + epitopes;
scNT-seq/scNT-seq2 + proteins; TimeLapse + CITE; and exact primary-paper titles.
Follow-up searches located original CITE-seq/scNT-seq Methods, the 2025 comparative
study, SOM3B's CITE-seq discussion, dsb and the 4sU perturbation study. Reviews were
used for discovery only, not as experimental proof in the six entries above.

Access was mixed: the SOM3B publisher HTML was directly readable. For several PMC
and Nature pages, direct open returned a browser challenge/login redirect while
search-indexed primary-article text exposed relevant method/results sections.
Bibliographic identity and scoped statements were checked against those primary
records, but this memo does not claim complete independent downloads of all full
texts or inspection of every supplement. No numerical optimum, exhaustive prior-art
clearance, or empirical success of a proposed combined assay is established here.
