Observed versus inferred
The parent monitor reports a coordinator still researching at approximately
15 minutes, using atlas/web retrieval and converting a primary PDF. Those actions
are allowed and can help choose informed expert mandates. They do not establish
that the coordinator misunderstood its role or that the following mismatch caused
the latency. No completed coordinator artifact was inspected in this audit.

Concrete mismatch
build_prompt concatenates the whole COMMON block before every role (prompts.py:365).
COMMON says "You are proposing an assay architecture" (18–19), requires a physical
design change (21–22), and instructs candidate id = expert_id plus an assessment
ledger over every graph record (58–74). These are proposal-author instructions.
PLAN instead forbids writing experts' final proposals and asks for ResearchPlan
(157–164). run.py maps plan to ResearchPlan and supplies no expert_id in that stage.
Literature and review stages receive the same author instructions despite producing
LiteratureReport and ReviewPanel. Their later role instructions counteract the
conflict, but do not remove it. Strict output schemas can reject the wrong final
artifact; they cannot prevent unnecessary design research before that artifact.

Smallest next-release correction
1. Describe the pipeline goal in COMMON, then explicitly say that the current
   role/schema determines what this agent produces. Keep shared evidence rules,
   uncertainty handling, exact references, graph meanings and tool usage.
2. Put candidate-authoring instructions (new workflow, expert_id, complete
   assessment ledger, sequencing readout) in a shared design-output block included
   only for propose/revise. Reviewing roles should inspect those properties in
   existing candidates, not produce a replacement graph.
3. Clarify PLAN's completion condition: a faithful measurement objective and two
   scientifically distinct, actionable expert mandates, with assumptions/questions
   carried forward. Research when needed to distinguish those mandates; resolution
   of every primitive/interface and full candidate prior-art adjudication belong
   to later roles. This is a responsibility boundary, not a paper/time cap.

Keep the present run as evidence
Do not rewrite its prompt mid-run. Inspect its completed plan, actual retrieved
sources and stage timing before attributing latency to this conflict. A later
same-request/same-snapshot comparison can test the narrower wording. Add prompt
assembly checks that non-author stages omit the design-output block and every
stage retains source/provenance rules; such tests verify wiring, not model behavior.
