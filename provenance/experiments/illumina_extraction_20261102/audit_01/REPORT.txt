Illumina first-pass extraction audit — 3 November 2026

What was actually processed
The first pass processed 478 source occurrences: 154 website cards, 285 main
poster rows and 39 footer/product/readout panels. This is not a count of unique
technologies. Baseline outcomes were 428 supported, 33 research_needed and 17 failed.
The batch processed every selected source and stopped with explicit unresolved
records; it did not crash partway through the queue.

The dispatcher wrongly used source occurrences as independent extraction work
units, without first consolidating established method/version identity. Drop-seq
received 3 extractions, G&T-seq 4 and ATAC-seq 2. Preserving each source was needed;
repeating full extraction for established duplicate depictions was unnecessary.
No validated fresh count of distinct methods exists yet. Name normalization or
the older 201-entity inventory cannot establish that count. Nuc-Seq is a useful
negative example: DNA and RNA methods share that name and must not be merged.

Publication references are a separate many-to-many relation. The registries have
879 distinct PubMed IDs and 2,275 source-record/PMID pairs. Highest registered level
per PMID: 268 fulltext, 607 abstract, 4 metadata. These figures do not mean that 879 full
papers were read or that 879 method associations were verified. A method can cite
its originating work, adaptations, applications and reviews. Repeating that
reference across a card and several posters increases occurrence counts again.
Exact examples and count boundaries: IDENTITY_ACCOUNTING.json.

Scope of the audit
Every 478 record was inspected mechanically, including candidate/review binding,
source IDs and quoted text, declared Operation I/O versus Flow endpoints, graph
copies, checkpoint outcomes and native execution traces. 2,582 distinct custody
files were rehashed. Under the original rules, none of the 428 accepted records
had a mechanical integrity failure. The durable baseline backup was independently
rehashed and matched its original SHA256 receipt.

All 17 failed and 33 held records were examined and assigned an explicit remedy in
triage.csv. Accepted records were also screened for recurring evidence risks,
with targeted source/scientific/visual inspection. This is not a new independent
scientific review of every assertion in the 428 accepted records.

Why the 17 failed
14: PaperMatch rejected explicitly unverified secondary catalogue references.
    This was a mismatch between the requested bibliographic accounting and the
    allowed source role, rather than 14 invented publications.
 2: provider blocks on viscRNA-seq and CirSeq. These remain recorded; no evasive
    retry or prompt workaround was attempted.
 1: Drop-ChIP exited 0 but did not deliver artifact.json. The archived final text
    reports repeated write-approval timeouts. Process success was not delivery.

Inspection of all 14 failed-stage artifacts also found two qualifications: the
OS-Seq catalogue-only entries carry method membership, and the catalogue scRC-Seq
entry uses relationship=unresolved. The narrow new rule deliberately does not
accept those unchanged artifacts. A fresh author must correct the source role
or retrieve primary evidence; the audit does not claim 14 automatic repairs.

Why the 33 held
18: member-specific applicability in grouped schematics. For example, the shared
    Phi29 amplification core does not establish that IMS-MDA uses the depicted
    finishing S1 treatment. Shared purpose also does not establish preparation
    identity for ProP-PD and PDZ-Seq.
 8: missing registered publication evidence or a missing method-specific paper
    association. A worker sometimes found the needed external passage, but the
    supplied source registry was immutable and could not admit it. EpiGnome also
    exposes a policy question: a commercial kit should not be assigned an
    unrelated journal paper just to satisfy a blanket paper requirement.
 3: source discrepancy needing primary/supplemental evidence: BrdU-CLIP order,
    MPE reagent identity and RNA Nuc-Seq fixation/implementation.
 2: consequential measurement interfaces: scAba-Seq immediate IVT product versus
    the depicted DNA endpoint; UMI-4C original-molecule identity carrier/linkage.
 2: host-policy holds with blank explanations despite all positive model checks.
    Both Ig/Rep/MAF depictions lack a non-background Rep-Seq paper association.

These categories are not generic missing buffer recipes. They affect attribution,
material history or the interpretation of the measurement. No missing source is
declared impossible to obtain. A registered full-text lead already exists for the
held Ψ-seq member in another record; it must be registered and independently used,
not resolved by copying that record's verdict.

Evidence failure found in accepted records
Exact quotation checking did not ensure that the quote supported the claim.
Seven scientific corrections in 3 records cite only an article title as primary
support: 4C(2),DeepCAGE(4),MPE-seq(1). Some correction rationales mention external
full-text passages that are absent from the registered sources. The separate
reviewer accepted those same inadequate evidence objects. This is an evidence
admission failure; it is not proof that the scientific correction itself is false.

Reopening all 478 records under stronger gates rejects those 3 records and a fourth:
the NOMe-Seq poster labels a background publication content_checked but cites only
its title. That fourth finding concerns a bibliographic evidence-status overclaim,
not an established error in the assay mechanism. Do not reinterpret baseline 428
as 428 records passing the new gates. The exact revalidation report is preserved.

Implemented general changes
- A catalogue mention may be retained only as an unassigned listed_not_read
  annotation. It cannot establish primary content, satisfy method coverage or
  create a publication node. This also prevents separate bibliography entries
  on one catalogue page from becoming one supposed publication.
- An article title can establish identity but cannot support a scientific
  correction or a content_checked status. The rule applies across methods.
- A single function now produces the scientific host outcome's hold reasons;
  self-check distinguishes structural validity from full scientific acceptance.
- Query-based retrieval now searches registered texts for actual matching
  passages. A miss returns found=false and no unrelated introductory text.
- Native execution failure, missing artifact and invalid artifact have separate
  failure categories.
- Optional structured final-response transport was implemented and its simple
  JSON probe passed with CLI 0.163.0, gpt-6.1-sol/xhigh. Full extraction/review
  schema transport has not been migrated or proven with this probe.

Validation
385 related software checks passed locally, in the isolated packaged runtime and
on Qiu. Tests include source-role negative controls and failed evidence admission.
Job 50936553 completed successfully in 15m46s using frozen runtime_06_audit.
All three fresh author/reviewer tests passed and published their source graphs,
with no publication errors or author revision: 3'-Seq, ORGANIC and MPE-seq.
The six native sessions have distinct thread IDs. Exact outputs, stages and
candidate/review hashes are retained under forward_examples/ and in the remote
job archive. The source packets were unchanged; prior extractions were not inputs.

3'-Seq retained its extra catalogue citation as listed_not_read and unassigned,
instead of failing the source-type check. ORGANIC likewise retained five secondary
citations without converting them into verified primary associations. Its
Kasinathan citation remains unread; a registered native-chromatin paper supports
the scoped architecture. This does not establish historical attribution.

MPE-seq omitted the old unsupported reagent correction. It preserves the named
addition, leaves its mechanism null and records a source-recovery question. The
reviewer accepted this as nonblocking for a faithful diagram-level record. Graph
retrieval returns that Unknown with the affected Operation. Its chemical role
therefore remains unresolved for downstream protocol design. Passing review has
not supplied the missing chemistry. VALIDATION_BEHAVIOR.json records this boundary.

Author plus reviewer duration was 14m29s for 3'-Seq, 11m59s for ORGANIC and 10m48s
for MPE-seq, running concurrently. Each used bounded source retrieval and
self-checks. These three development tests do not establish a general performance
or accuracy gain. Their results are separate from the baseline graph counts.

Baseline graph republication completed inside that allocation: 424 supported,

GRAPH_PUBLICATION.json records graph paths, hashes, input pins and exclusions.

Before another full batch
1. Establish reviewed method/version work units, with all applicable source
   depictions attached. Preserve grouped/ambiguous source membership explicitly;
   do not merge by label, hash or shared citation alone.
2. Recover the question-specific missing evidence into new source packets, then
   run targeted author/reviewer attempts. The current read-only retrieval helper
   cannot itself acquire and register these documents.
3. Use revalidated graph outputs and inspect new evidence-admission failures.
   Original graph files remain historical artifacts.
4. Test whole-schema structured delivery before replacing file-based extraction.

Remaining limits
Neither exact quotes nor two model sessions prove scientific entailment. The
title-only guard closes one observed failure; an irrelevant body passage could
still be misused. Semantic scientific review remains necessary. No new historical
ancestry, biochemical interchangeability or cross-method equivalence is admitted
by this audit. The method/version consolidation gate is specified, not yet wired
into the batch scheduler. This is an audited source-architecture extraction pass,
not a completed deduplicated evolutionary technology graph.

Primary implementation references and rationale are recorded in
docs/decisions/2026-11-03-extraction-audit.txt. Full per-record questions and
recovery leads remain in recovery_requests.json; triage.csv is the compact worklist.
