2 November: methods-collection source roles (inspection; not a deployed change)

Rechecked the supplied DNA/RNA collection PDFs, their prior figure-coverage audit,
current card_sources() implementation, and the completed ATAC/ChIPmentation pair.
The collections are already acquired and figure-indexed. That does not mean their
method-specific pages are systematically supplied to every extraction: the current
card_sources() builds catalogue text, selected diagram images, publication metadata,
and available paper texts. It has no automatic collection-section lookup. The pair's
registered text sources contain catalogue entries and papers, not collection pages.

Proposed next source adapter: attach the relevant collection section (prose, figure,
caption, advantages/limitations, and reference roles) to an existing scoped method.
Retain document edition, page/region and source identity. A repeated poster/card/PDF
drawing is another source occurrence, not an independent scientific corroboration,
new method, or historical edge. Shared titles and adjacent placement are retrieval
leads; they do not establish identity or descent. Application/review citations and
associated-kit lists must not become method-origin or mandatory-reagent assertions.

Concrete comparison-relevant source discrepancy identified during this inspection:
DNA collection p11 depicts and describes restriction digestion, adapter addition,
then a second restriction digestion. Its cited original Peterson et al. 2012 paper
(DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0037135, PMC3365034), Methods / Double Digest Restriction
Associated DNA (ddRAD) Sequencing, explicitly describes two enzymes simultaneously.
The collection page was visually inspected from its pinned local PDF; the original
main-text Methods were read online. This is a workflow-order discrepancy for the
original implementation, not a missing execution setting. No accepted extraction
or frozen run was edited. Any future original-ddRAD graph must resolve this using
source-qualified Correction records and review; first inspect whether its current
extraction already addresses it rather than assuming an existing graph is wrong.

RNA p110 (Drop-seq) distinguishes capture inside droplets from cDNA synthesis after
bead recovery. RNA p14 (cP-RNA-Seq) explains why terminal chemistry affects selection.
These illustrate useful prose/diagram alignment without full recipe extraction.
