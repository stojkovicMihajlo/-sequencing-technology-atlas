The canonical category/subclassification determines placement, not ancestry.
Additional classifications link to the same folder; shared files are linked
rather than duplicated. Original Illumina placements and printed poster
sections remain distinguishable from explicit curator assignments. Being on
the DNA poster does not automatically mean Genome; presence in two categories
does not establish a joint measurement.

Paper association is not workflow confirmation
A repeated depiction belongs to the existing method. Two named methods can
share a drawing or paper without being duplicates. A shared card description
can describe a family rather than each individual member; keep that qualification
visible instead of calling it a method-specific protocol. A protocol publication,
an original journal article and a foundational component paper can support
different portions/versions. Preserve those roles and scope. Recovered DamID
and MIRA papers use microarray readout; they do not confirm the entire sequencing
workflow. MATQ and CRISPR-UMI author protocols are separate publications;
obtaining them does not recover the parent journal article.
