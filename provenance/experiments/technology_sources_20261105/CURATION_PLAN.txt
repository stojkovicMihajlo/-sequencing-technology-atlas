What belongs in a curated entry now
1. Technology name, aliases, and the version/implementation the entry covers.
   Repeated depictions do not create additional technologies. Shared papers do
   not establish that names denote the same technology. Grouped cards retain
   all their named methods without assuming identical protocols.
2. Illumina's original catalogue placements and printed poster sections, with
   their sources. A combined-category view links back to the same technology
   folder. Being on the DNA poster does not automatically mean Genome: that
   poster also contains epigenetic methods. Being on two posters does not
   automatically establish a joint measurement.
3. Associated papers, explicitly distinguishing the introducing method paper,
   a later protocol/version, supplements, related literature and unverified
   candidates. A card bibliography alone does not establish the role of every
   cited paper; this needs curation. Record DOI/PMID, publication/version dates,
   and the evidence for the method association.
4. Actual available files: original HTML/CMS description, original diagram,
   full shared poster, paper, supplements and published protocol, as available.
   Shared files are stored once and linked into every relevant method folder.
5. Review status: identity, classification, paper association, file acquisition,
   and figure readability. Download success is not scientific verification.

Folders in the source collection
technologies/<technology>/
  method.json       source names, classifications and source/document bindings
  catalogue/        original description and provenance
  diagrams/         actual source images/crops, if available
  posters/          links to complete shared posters
  guides/           links to complete methods guides
  papers/           explicitly listed references; paper roles still need review
  related_papers/   separately identified related publications
  paper_candidates/ unverified method-paper associations
  index.html        human-readable view of those same source records
groups/
  Genome/
  Transcriptome/
  Epigenome/
  Combined catalogue categories/<category combination>/
  Category review pending/
classification/     all original catalogue paths and printed poster sections
shared/             single stored copies of source documents

The groups/ entries are directory links, so a second classification does not
duplicate a technology, its papers, or a future extraction job. The original
Illumina categories remain unchanged. A future scientific measurement category
can differ and must be explicitly marked as a curator assignment with evidence.
For example, Illumina lists scM&T-seq under Genome and Transcriptome; its primary
paper describes methylome plus transcriptome measurements:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26752769/
