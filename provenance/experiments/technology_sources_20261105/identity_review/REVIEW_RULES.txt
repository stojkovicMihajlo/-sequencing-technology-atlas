Unit of organization
One named method, scoped to its described measurement and implementation lineage.
An alternative spelling is not another method. A separately named implementation
is retained when the cited work describes a distinct workflow/target or version;
retaining it does not claim independent invention or mechanistic uniqueness.
A broad family, generic application overview, reusable component, or commercial
kit is explicitly typed and is not counted as a distinct named assay.

Evidence rules
- Read the catalogue prose, printed row, and method-labelled bibliography together.
- Merge on explicit synonym evidence within that source context, not punctuation,
  shared image, shared paper, same application, or common molecular mechanism alone.
- Split homonyms using the measured material and publication, retaining source IDs.
- A source bibliography is not a per-method bibliography. Papers on sibling methods
  stay in source_references; only selected associations go in method_references.
- A correction must preserve the original statement and identify its replacement
  and basis. Identity review is not a claim of full protocol/source verification.
- Preserve independently published variants even if Illumina uses one cartoon.
- A label that cannot be resolved is retained outside the extraction queue, with
  the exact failed question, not invented into an assay to make counts add up.
