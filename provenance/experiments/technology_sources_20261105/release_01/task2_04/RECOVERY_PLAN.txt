TASK 2: FINISH THE METHOD SOURCE LIBRARY

Scope: the existing 273 Illumina named methods, including all 99 poster-only
entries. No new method, workflow extraction, historical edge or model call is
authorized by this source-acquisition stage.

1. Reuse the current source vault without rewriting its records or files.
2. Check the 62 still-missing assigned publications against the four declared
   earlier source registries. Reuse only an original article whose recorded
   checksum, own title, own PMID/DOI and substantive body all pass checks.
   A bibliography mention or derived model text is not an original article.
3. Try the newly found, explicitly listed public PDF locations. Do not repeat
   unchanged public routes for every paper. Preserve all failures.
4. Collect the original authors' MATQ-seq and CRISPR-UMI protocols as separately
   identified publications. Their titles, DOIs and source associations were
   inspected. They do not replace the unavailable journal articles or prove
   that a later implementation uses identical conditions.
5. Republish a new source-folder snapshot. Keep 273 method folders and their
   original sources/classifications; carry forward the exact rejected-image
   findings and the two guide-page visual scope reviews.
6. Verify all generated links/source hashes, publish one exact remaining-paper
   list, archive before changing the stable library pointer, and record the
   result in TASKS.csv and RESUME.txt.
