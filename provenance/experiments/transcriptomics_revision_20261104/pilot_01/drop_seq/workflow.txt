Record: drop_seq
Scope: One Drop-Seq cell/bead droplet implementation repeated in the website diagram and the two selected poster rows. The left yellow-highlighted poly(A) RNA is the biological target; Cell suspension and Each bead with unique oligos are starting materials, not unshown preparation actions. Four bead-primer labels distinguish oligo(dT), molecular index/UMI, cell label and universal handle. The retained route is co-encapsulation → lysis/capture → pooled bead recovery → template-switching cDNA synthesis, PCR and Nextera XT library preparation → sequencing. The output represents captured single-cell mRNA through barcoded 3′-end cDNA tags and UMI-aware digital gene expression. Only the Drop-Seq rows are targets: neighboring CytoSeq, CLaP and Hi-SCL rows, similar-method lists, and review applications do not expand this scope. No separately routed branches appear within the selected rows.
