Research and code audit — 19 October 2026

Decision: preserve every method-specific State and Operation. Add separately reviewed links to reusable definitions. Exact hashing tracks identity/provenance; semantic retrieval finds comparison candidates; evidence-backed contextual review decides the kind of relation. None alone proves experimental substitutability.

Current implementation
The published cohort21 graph has201 technology nodes,10 workflows,292 operations,385 states and58 modules. It retains local workflow namespaces. There is no common-operation concept overlay in that publication. Existing historical connections are imported separately.
SHA256 pins original bytes and canonical serialized records; the workflow namespace binds its Workflow, source packet and review. This is not semantic hashing.
Optional graph discovery combines lexical and local MiniLM rankings. The shared registry's separate proposal queue is lexical, with an exhaustive small-catalogue fallback. The dense retriever is not integrated into that queue.
A separately invoked common-definition registry, source review, duplicate proposals, field comparisons and atomic publication already exist. They have bounded development tests, not full-atlas scientific validation. SQLite coordinates short commits and exact-pair leases; the whole catalogue is not locked during model reasoning.

Representation to prespecify
State: a source/variant-scoped description of material or data available at a workflow interface. It is not a reusable material class or a claim of an observed experimental sample. Mixtures and assemblies are permitted; identical chemistry in an aliquot and production branch still requires separate lineage.
Operation: a source-described transformation, handling action or measurement within that workflow. Its granularity must preserve consequential interfaces; it need not be the smallest reaction or text sentence.
Ask consistently what enters/leaves, what changes, what information/identity survives, where fractions go, what context matters, and the exact scope. Attach status and evidence per assertion: reported, inferred, unknown, or not assessed; distinguish supported absence from missing information and separate applicability. These richer comparative facets are a proposed extension, not present uniformly in accepted records.
Split operations only when a source-supported intermediate or handoff is needed to preserve a consequential distinction. Coupled transformations and repeated programs can remain composite. Never invent hidden intermediates merely to align methods.

Minimal proposed process
1.Extract source-specific workflows without forcing existing shared vocabulary.
2.Profile input/output states and connected operation neighborhoods using the same questions.
3.Retrieve a union of lexical, semantic and facet-based candidate definitions. A shortlist is a search result, not proof of completeness.
4.Compare nearby alternatives with sources. Keep equivalent scope, broader/narrower, shared purpose, shared mechanism and contextual substitutability separate. Permit unresolved and no match. Similarity is not transitive.
5.Require registry search before publishing a new shared definition. Preserve local records regardless of whether a matching concept exists. Queue unmatched definitions as proposals and compare them with concurrent proposals.
6.Use short version-checked atomic commits after source review. Revisit affected earlier mappings after registry refinement. A write lock prevents conflicting writes, not undetected semantic duplicates.

How to evaluate
Hold the reviewer constant and compare label retrieval, full-text hybrid retrieval, and structured contextual retrieval. Evaluate retrieval misses separately using a bounded exhaustive subset. Include paraphrases, same names with different meanings, purpose-sharing mechanism differences, material-interface differences, composite-versus-split descriptions, rare concepts, unknown properties, and shuffled insertion/candidate order. Report false merges, false splits, duplicates, relation accuracy, interface preservation and latency. Late independent expert review is needed for scientific accuracy; software tests and model agreement are not its substitute. A suggestion-blind expert subset reduces the risk that model decisions become their own evaluation labels.
