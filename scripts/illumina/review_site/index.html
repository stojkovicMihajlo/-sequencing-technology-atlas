Needed for a method atlas

Review which depictions describe the same method or distinct versions. Preserve source differences. Add paper-supported development claims bound to the Operations that changed. The paired comparison is one completed example on earlier records.

What to check yourself

Start with Drop-seq. Does the workflow preserve the information needed to explain the assay? Can you locate evidence for an Operation without searching another file? Then open the paired comparison: does it clearly distinguish what is shared, what changed and what remains uncertain?

Use in assay design

  1. Start with the desired measurement and specimen.
  2. Retrieve relevant workflows and inspect connected Operations, their input/output States and evidence.
  3. Follow reviewed design changes to find alternatives and trade-offs.
  4. Propose a change, naming which Operations and interfaces would change.
  5. Retrieve missing protocol details and verify the proposed interfaces against primary sources.

Similarity proposes comparisons. It does not establish ancestry or interchangeability. This site is a review view, not an invention engine.
