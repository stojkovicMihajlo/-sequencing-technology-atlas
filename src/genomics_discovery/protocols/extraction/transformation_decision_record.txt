Transformation comparison decision record, 2026-10-14

Contract and material ambiguity
Inputs are a frozen extracted Workflow, its registered source packet, an explicit
operation selection and optional bound workflow review. The object is a complete
declared transformation occurrence with contextual interfaces, not a keyword tag.
A reusable definition and its memberships are further scientific interpretations.
Same words, graph connectivity, source quote matches and shared purpose cannot
identify shared mechanism, substitutability, historical ancestry or completeness.
These are semantic-object choices, analogous to law-level ambiguity in the audit
skill; no probabilistic estimator, training law or performance claim is introduced.

Construction and limits
Reuse StrictModel, Workflow, State, Operation, Flow, Gap, Evidence and canonical
digest/provenance/review gates. A connected selected unit retains full raw states,
actions, resources, information effects, external interfaces and whole-workflow
unknowns. Definitions state substrate, operation, physical mechanism or explicit
unknown, output, purpose, information effect and contextual requirements.
Membership proposals cite exact occurrence hashes and their packet passages.
An independent source-first membership review checks each member, with evidence
covering its declared interfaces and selected actions. Host validation verifies
bindings, inspection and quotes; the reviewer judges entailment and applicability.
Supported membership remains a model-reviewed comparison candidate. Underlying
workflow review status is retained, including needs_revision/research_needed;
neither membership support nor an operation cut promotes a production record.

Falsifiers and next discriminator
Reject missing facets, unknown mechanisms used as positive mechanism identity,
disconnected or unknown operation selections, stale workflow/packet/member hashes,
missing/duplicate/unsupported membership checks and ungrounded evidence. Broad
word overlap alone produces no memberships. Test shared-purpose and shared-
mechanism declarations separately, preserving distinct source contexts. A real
cross-method test requires a second independently extracted workflow and fresh
membership review; the one-method tagmentation demonstration cannot establish it.
