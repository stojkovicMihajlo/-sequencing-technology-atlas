EVIDENCE RECOVERY CLARIFICATION — user instruction,2026-10-08
This supplements the frozen extraction contract; it does not rewrite historical prompts or reviews.

Actively resolve missing information before treating it as a limitation of the published method. An uninspected supplement, failed URL, omitted deposited-data record, or unread named dependency is unfinished curation work. Search the original publisher's article and comments/corrections, supplements and figures, original data/code archives and cited protocols. Follow a broken original link through legitimate publisher/author/repository alternatives. Record actual searches so another worker can continue; never claim impossibility from absence in one source.

Use context to resolve apparent differences before recording a scientific contradiction. Check the same experiment, sample/cohort, assay branch, processing stage, units, stock versus final concentration, master-mix versus aliquot volume, rounded versus exact quantities, and original versus later corrected version. Different contexts are not contradictory author claims. Explicit later author statements about the original experiment can resolve historical details, while their newly modified recipe must remain separately attributed.

A missing experiment-specific execution log is not automatically a missing published protocol instruction. Extract the written recipe accurately. Do not invent a per-sample override merely because the paper also describes a pilot or optimization step.

Distinguish unfinished retrieval, a detail genuinely not reported after documented relevant searches, and mutually incompatible source statements about the same pinned operation. A shared ambiguous/conflicting label must not be read as proof that the authors contradicted themselves. Preserve specific unresolved evidence requests for continued work; do not promote a critical unread dependency to a completed extraction.

Independent source review means the extracted claims were checked against their cited evidence. It does not by itself establish that retrieval is exhaustive, all recipes are complete, or the assay has been reproduced. Communicate these dimensions separately.
